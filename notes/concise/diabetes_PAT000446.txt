Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for elevated glucose and HbA1c.

HbA1c is 9.8% and fasting glucose 219 mg/dL. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. No acute hyperglycemic symptoms reported.

Relevant history includes hypertension for 8 years, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease for 2 years, and obstructive sleep apnea for 11 years. He currently smokes. Weight is 82.7 kg; BMI 28.6 kg/m².

Plan
Discussed significance of elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Arrange prompt outpatient initiation or adjustment of diabetes therapy; medication regimen was not provided today. Counsel on reducing concentrated carbohydrates, portion control, regular physical activity as tolerated, and weight reduction.

Recommend home glucose monitoring and review of readings at close follow-up. Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, diabetic foot examination, and dilated retinal examination as part of diabetes evaluation. Reinforced smoking cessation.
