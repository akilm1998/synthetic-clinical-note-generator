Chief Complaint

Initial consultation for management of type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Justin Richardson is a 73-year-old man with a 14-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, currently without reported diabetes-related symptoms or complications. He denies polydipsia, polyuria, polyphagia, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the extremities. He has a 15-year history of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease. He currently smokes. Diabetes treatment history is not provided.

Objective

Height 169 cm; weight 67.4 kg; BMI 23.6 kg/m². Blood pressure 134/81 mmHg, pulse 79 bpm, respiratory rate 14/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 95%.

Hemoglobin A1c 9.3%. Fasting glucose 192 mg/dL.

Assessment

1. Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications. Glycemic control is above goal, with A1c 9.3% and fasting glucose 192 mg/dL. Despite the absence of hyperglycemic symptoms, these values indicate clinically significant persistent hyperglycemia. No neuropathic, infectious, constitutional, or visual symptoms are reported.

2. Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, longstanding, reported duration 15 years.

3. Tobacco use. Active smoking increases cardiovascular and microvascular risk in the setting of diabetes.

Plan

Discussed the importance of active diabetes management, nutrition focused on reduced refined carbohydrate intake, regular physical activity as tolerated, and home glucose monitoring.

Recommend initiation or optimization of glucose-lowering therapy after review of renal function, hepatic status, contraindications, and treatment preferences; metformin would be a reasonable first-line consideration if appropriate. Given the degree of hyperglycemia, timely pharmacologic treatment and close reassessment are warranted.

Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, and comprehensive diabetic foot and retinal assessments as part of complication screening. Counsel regarding daily foot inspection and prompt evaluation of wounds or infection.

Smoking cessation counseling provided, including discussion of its importance for cardiovascular risk reduction. Follow up after laboratory and screening evaluation to review results and adjust therapy.
