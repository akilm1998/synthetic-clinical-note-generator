Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective

Anna Cook is a 60-year-old woman, 180 cm and 67.7 kg (BMI 20.9 kg/m²), seen for follow-up of longstanding anemia present for approximately 14 years. She reports headache and appears pale but denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, dyspnea, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. Anemia has not been actively managed. Relevant chronic conditions include malignancy for 14 years, inflammatory bowel disease for 9 years, and chronic infection for 4 years. She currently smokes.

Objective

Vital signs: BP 132/77 mmHg, pulse 87 bpm, respirations 19/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Pallor is present. Cardiovascular and respiratory examinations are otherwise unremarkable.

Laboratory results: Hemoglobin 9.2 g/dL, hematocrit 28.4%, RBC 3.55 million/µL, MCV 80 fL, serum iron 29 µg/dL, ferritin 331 ng/mL, TIBC 206 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 14.1%.

Assessment

Chronic anemia consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/disease. The low hemoglobin and hematocrit with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, reduced TIBC, and elevated ferritin support an inflammatory pattern rather than uncomplicated iron depletion. Contributing chronic inflammatory conditions include malignancy, inflammatory bowel disease, and chronic infection. She is hemodynamically stable with limited symptoms aside from headache and pallor.

Plan

Obtain repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, along with vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia. Continue evaluation and management of the underlying malignancy, inflammatory bowel disease, and chronic infection with the appropriate treating clinicians. Consider iron replacement strategy, including intravenous iron if clinically indicated, based on reassessment and underlying disease activity; erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy would require specialist-directed consideration. Review for evidence of active blood loss in the context of inflammatory bowel disease and malignancy. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Arrange outpatient follow-up after repeat laboratory evaluation, with earlier assessment for worsening headache, dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, or bleeding.
