Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes.

Subjective
Madeline Warren is a 56-year-old woman with a 22-year history of type 2 diabetes and obesity of approximately 12 years’ duration. She is not currently receiving documented diabetes management. She denies polydipsia, polyuria, polyphagia, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. Reports tingling in the feet; denies documented numbness. She does not smoke.

Objective
Height 169 cm, weight 101.1 kg, BMI 35.4 kg/m². BP 119/78 mmHg, pulse 82/min, respirations 20/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 99% on room air. HbA1c 9.4%; fasting glucose 248 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently inadequately controlled based on HbA1c and fasting glucose. Patient is largely asymptomatic but reports distal tingling concerning for possible early peripheral neuropathic symptoms. Obesity contributes to cardiometabolic risk.

Plan
Discussed importance of active diabetes management, nutrition focused on reduced refined carbohydrates, portion control, weight reduction, and regular physical activity as tolerated. Recommend initiation or adjustment of glucose-lowering therapy, with consideration of metformin if clinically appropriate, after medication and renal function review. Recommend home glucose monitoring and review of readings at follow-up.

Obtain urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, and perform a comprehensive diabetic foot examination; recommend routine dilated retinal evaluation. Counsel on daily foot inspection and prompt evaluation of wounds or progressive sensory changes. Follow up soon to reassess glycemic control and treatment response.
