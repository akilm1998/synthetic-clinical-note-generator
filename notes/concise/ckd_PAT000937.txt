Chief Complaint
Annual review and chronic kidney disease follow-up.

Subjective
Sadie Hunter is a 54-year-old woman presenting for comprehensive annual review. Chronic kidney disease has been present for approximately 10 years and has not been actively managed. Reports fatigue. Denies pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, peripheral edema, change in urination, sleep disturbance, or shortness of breath. Known secondary hyperparathyroidism for 8 years. Nonsmoker.

Objective
Height 159 cm, weight 65.5 kg, BMI 25.9 kg/m². BP 137/89 mmHg, pulse 70/min, respirations 16/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Serum creatinine 5.88 mg/dL, eGFR 7 mL/min/1.73 m², BUN 71 mg/dL.

Assessment
Chronic kidney disease with markedly reduced renal function and elevated nitrogenous waste markers. Fatigue may be related to advanced renal dysfunction. Currently no reported symptoms of fluid overload or overt uremic prodrome. Secondary hyperparathyroidism, longstanding. Blood pressure mildly elevated. Overall renal disease requires prompt specialist assessment and active management.

Plan
Arrange urgent nephrology evaluation for comprehensive CKD management and discussion of renal replacement planning as clinically indicated. Obtain renal panel with electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, and intact parathyroid hormone; consider renal ultrasound. Review renal-safe medication use and avoid NSAIDs and other nephrotoxins. Counsel on monitoring blood pressure, maintaining an appropriate renal diet, and reporting reduced urine output, worsening fatigue, nausea/vomiting, confusion, chest pain, or shortness of breath immediately.

Continue annual preventive care, including age- and sex-appropriate cancer screening, immunizations, cardiovascular risk assessment, and routine health maintenance. Follow up promptly after nephrology assessment and laboratory review.
