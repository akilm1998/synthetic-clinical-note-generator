Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Brianna Gibson is a 77-year-old woman, nonsmoker, here for follow-up of anemia present for approximately 24 months. She has a longstanding malignancy reported for 15 years. She denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, pallor, shortness of breath, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. No current anemia-directed medication is documented.

Objective
Height 156 cm, weight 60.8 kg, BMI 25.0 kg/m². BP 136/77 mmHg, pulse 87 bpm, respirations 19/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Recent laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.3 g/dL, hematocrit 24.2%, RBC 2.72 million/µL, MCV 89 fL, serum iron 24 µg/dL, ferritin 300 ng/mL, TIBC 250 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 9.6%.

Assessment
Anemia of chronic disease/inflammation, persistent and currently untreated. Normocytic anemia with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and low-normal TIBC, consistent with iron-restricted erythropoiesis in the setting of chronic disease/malignancy. Despite significant anemia, she is presently asymptomatic with stable vital signs.

Plan
Obtain follow-up CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear. Check renal function, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers as clinically indicated to evaluate contributing factors. Review for occult blood loss and nutritional causes. Coordinate management with the clinician overseeing her chronic malignancy; consider hematology evaluation if anemia persists or worsens. Iron replacement, intravenous iron, or an erythropoiesis-stimulating agent should be considered only after review of the underlying disease and additional evaluation. Advise urgent evaluation for new shortness of breath, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, marked weakness, or bleeding. Reassess after repeat laboratory testing.
