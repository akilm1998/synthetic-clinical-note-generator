Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective
Joseph Bailey is a 60-year-old male presenting for follow-up of anemia present for approximately 13 years. He reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, palpitations, shortness of breath, or reduced exercise tolerance. No pallor has been noted. Relevant chronic conditions include inflammatory bowel disease for 9 years, malignancy for 2 years, and chronic infection for 15 years. He does not smoke. Current anemia-directed medications are not documented.

Objective
Height 186 cm, weight 82.3 kg, BMI 23.8 kg/m². BP 132/79 mmHg, pulse 82 bpm, respiratory rate 19/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Hemoglobin 9.1 g/dL, hematocrit 27.0%, RBC 3.33 million/µL, MCV 81 fL. Serum iron 34 µg/dL, ferritin 159 ng/mL, TIBC 209 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 16.3%.

Assessment
Chronic anemia consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/disease. The anemia is currently asymptomatic and predominantly normocytic, with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, preserved ferritin, and low TIBC, supporting impaired iron availability in the setting of chronic inflammatory disease, chronic infection, and malignancy.

Plan
Obtain interval CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, along with renal function, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers as clinically appropriate. Continue evaluation and management of the underlying inflammatory bowel disease, chronic infection, and malignancy, as control of these conditions is central to anemia management. Assess for concurrent iron deficiency or occult blood loss, particularly given inflammatory bowel disease; consider iron replacement or hematology input based on repeat studies and clinical course. Return for routine reassessment, with earlier evaluation for new dyspnea, chest discomfort, palpitations, dizziness, bleeding, or worsening exercise tolerance.
