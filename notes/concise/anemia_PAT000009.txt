Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Lydia Ford is a 39-year-old woman presenting for follow-up of anemia present for approximately 6 years. She reports headache but denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, shortness of breath, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. No tobacco use. Comorbidity includes heart failure diagnosed approximately 1 year ago. No current anemia-directed treatment is documented.

Objective
Height 157 cm, weight 68.0 kg, BMI 27.6 kg/m². BP 116/79 mmHg, pulse 86 bpm, RR 22/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Pallor noted. Cardiovascular and respiratory examinations are otherwise unremarkable.

Recent laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.6 g/dL, hematocrit 25.1%, RBC 3.14 million/µL, MCV 80 fL, serum iron 47 µg/dL, ferritin 219 ng/mL, TIBC 200 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 23.5%.

Assessment
Chronic anemia with normocytic indices and low serum iron/TIBC with preserved-elevated ferritin, most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation. Hemoglobin remains significantly reduced. Headache and pallor are present, without current cardiopulmonary symptoms or hemodynamic instability. Heart failure is a relevant comorbidity.

Plan
Obtain CBC with reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia and assess contributing chronic disease. Review for occult blood loss and other sources of inflammation at follow-up. Coordinate anemia management in the context of heart failure; consider hematology involvement if anemia persists or worsens. Advise prompt evaluation for new shortness of breath, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, or marked weakness. Reassess after laboratory evaluation.
