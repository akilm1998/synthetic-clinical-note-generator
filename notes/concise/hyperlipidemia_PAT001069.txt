Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for hyperlipidemia.

Subjective
Cody Warren is a 94-year-old man presenting for follow-up of hyperlipidemia identified approximately 2 years ago. The condition has not been under active management. He denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, dizziness, xanthomas, or xanthelasma. He continues to smoke. Longstanding obesity is noted, present for approximately 49 years.

Objective
Height 189 cm; weight 132.9 kg; BMI 37.2 kg/m².
BP 133/76 mmHg, pulse 80/min, respirations 19/min, temperature 36.7°C, SpO2 94%.
Recent lipid panel: total cholesterol 363 mg/dL, LDL-C 279 mg/dL, HDL-C 35 mg/dL, triglycerides 243 mg/dL.

Assessment
Hyperlipidemia, unspecified, with markedly elevated LDL-C and associated low HDL-C and hypertriglyceridemia. Despite absence of reported cardiovascular symptoms, the lipid profile confers substantial atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk, particularly in the setting of advanced age, tobacco use, and obesity.

Plan
Discuss initiation of lipid-lowering therapy, with treatment intensity individualized to overall health status, functional goals, and patient preference; review potential benefits and adverse effects before prescribing.
Recommend repeat fasting lipid panel and evaluation for potentially contributing secondary causes if clinically appropriate. Consider ApoB and lipoprotein(a) measurement for further risk characterization.
Counsel regarding reduction of saturated/trans fats, weight-conscious dietary changes, and activity as tolerated.
Provide smoking-cessation counseling.
Follow up after lipid reassessment to review response and finalize ongoing management.
