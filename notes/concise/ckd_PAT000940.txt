Chief Complaint
Initial consultation for chronic kidney disease.

Subjective
Teresa Cunningham is a 37-year-old woman, height 170 cm, weight 67.6 kg, BMI 23.4 kg/m², presenting for evaluation of chronic kidney disease reportedly present for 11 years. She has a 6-year history of hypertension. She denies fatigue, pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, sleep disturbance, peripheral edema, change in urination, and shortness of breath. Nonsmoker. No current medication regimen is provided.

Objective
Vital signs: BP 155/94 mmHg, pulse 71 bpm, respirations 15/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Laboratory data:
Creatinine 2.83 mg/dL
BUN 88 mg/dL
eGFR 15 mL/min/1.73 m²

Assessment
Chronic kidney disease with significant reduction in kidney function and marked azotemia, reportedly longstanding for 11 years. Currently without reported uremic or fluid-overload symptoms. Etiology is not established from the available history. Hypertension is above goal and likely contributes to ongoing renal risk.

Plan
Arrange prompt nephrology consultation for comprehensive CKD evaluation and management, including discussion of renal replacement planning if clinically indicated.

Recommend serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound.

Address blood pressure promptly with medication reconciliation and initiation or adjustment of kidney-appropriate antihypertensive therapy; encourage home BP monitoring.

Avoid NSAIDs and other potentially nephrotoxic agents; review all prescription, over-the-counter, and herbal medications before use. Counsel on appropriate hydration, sodium restriction, and prompt evaluation for nausea, vomiting, worsening fatigue, confusion, edema, reduced urine output, or shortness of breath.

Close outpatient follow-up after recommended testing and nephrology evaluation.
