Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Connor Walters is a 51-year-old man, nonsmoker, with a 10-year history of anemia in the setting of chronic kidney disease for 8 years and malignancy for 6 years. He denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, dyspnea, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. Pallor is noted. No current anemia-directed treatment is documented.

Objective
Height 164 cm; weight 57.3 kg; BMI 21.3 kg/m².
BP 130/88 mmHg, pulse 85 bpm, respiratory rate 21/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.
Pale appearance. No tachycardia or hypoxemia.

Laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.5 g/dL, hematocrit 26.1%, RBC 2.9 million/µL, MCV 90 fL, serum iron 30 µg/dL, ferritin 209 ng/mL, TIBC 208 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 14.4%.

Assessment
Chronic normocytic anemia consistent with anemia of chronic disease/inflammation, likely multifactorial in the setting of chronic kidney disease and malignancy. Iron-restricted profile with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated-range ferritin, and low TIBC. Currently clinically stable without reported cardiopulmonary or constitutional symptoms.

Plan
Recommend prompt review by the clinicians managing his chronic kidney disease and malignancy to address the persistent anemia and determine appropriateness of anemia-directed therapy, including consideration of iron replacement or an erythropoiesis-stimulating agent based on the underlying disease context.

Recommend CBC with reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers for further characterization if not recently assessed. Monitor hemoglobin and clinical status.

Seek urgent evaluation for new or worsening dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, dizziness, significant weakness, or bleeding.
