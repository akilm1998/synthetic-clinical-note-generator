Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

She denies polydipsia, polyuria, polyphagia, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. Reports numbness and tingling in the hands and feet. She does not smoke.

Height 180 cm; weight 74.2 kg; BMI 22.9 kg/m².
BP 153/92 mmHg, pulse 70 bpm, respiratory rate 15/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 97%.
HbA1c 9.8%; fasting glucose 231 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus with significant hyperglycemia, currently not adequately managed. Chronic distal numbness and tingling raise concern for peripheral neuropathic symptoms, although no wounds or infectious complications are reported.
Hypertension, above goal today.
Chronic kidney disease, duration 11 years; renal function data not provided.

Plan
Discussed the markedly elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose and the need for prompt initiation or intensification of a comprehensive diabetes treatment plan. Review current medications and adherence at the next medication reconciliation; select therapy with consideration of chronic kidney disease and renal function.
Recommend home glucose monitoring with documentation of readings, individualized nutrition counseling, and regular physical activity as tolerated.
Obtain serum creatinine/eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio to guide treatment and assess kidney involvement; fasting lipid profile is also recommended.
Perform comprehensive foot examination, including sensory assessment, and arrange dilated retinal evaluation. Counsel on daily foot inspection and prompt reporting of skin breakdown or infection.
Reassess blood pressure and review antihypertensive management, with attention to kidney-protective therapy when clinically appropriate.
Close outpatient follow-up after completion of recommended laboratory evaluation to reassess glycemic and blood pressure control.
