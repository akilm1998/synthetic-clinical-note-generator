Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Subjective

Denise Wheeler is a 72-year-old woman with a 14-year history of essential hypertension, currently without active management. She reports intermittent dizziness. Denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, or epistaxis. Comorbid type 2 diabetes mellitus for 32 years, chronic kidney disease for 6 years, and coronary artery disease for 6 years. Current smoker. No antihypertensive regimen reported.

Objective

BP 173/89 mmHg, pulse 80 bpm, respirations 16/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 96% on room air. Height 162 cm, weight 74.3 kg, BMI 28.3 kg/m². Serum sodium 138 mmol/L, potassium 4.2 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension, persistently uncontrolled, with associated dizziness. Markedly elevated office blood pressure without reported symptoms of acute end-organ injury. Higher cardiovascular and renal risk given diabetes, chronic kidney disease, coronary artery disease, and tobacco use.

Plan

Repeat blood pressure after seated rest and confirm with home readings if available. Arrange prompt treatment initiation and medication reconciliation, with regimen selection mindful of chronic kidney disease, diabetes, and coronary artery disease. Review dietary sodium reduction, weight management, physical activity as tolerated, and smoking cessation. Continue monitoring electrolytes and renal function with antihypertensive therapy. Seek emergency care for chest pain, shortness of breath, severe headache, new neurologic symptoms, vision changes, or markedly worsening dizziness. Close follow-up for blood pressure reassessment.
