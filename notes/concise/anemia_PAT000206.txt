Initial consultation for chronic anemia.

Laboratory Data
Hemoglobin 8.6 g/dL, hematocrit 26.0%, RBC 3.25 million/µL, MCV 80 fL. Serum iron 27 µg/dL, ferritin 363 ng/mL, TIBC 245 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 11%.

Assessment
Chronic anemia with significant reduction in hemoglobin and hematocrit. Indices are low-normal normocytic with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and reduced TIBC, a pattern most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/chronic disease, likely in the setting of longstanding heart failure. Concurrent absolute iron deficiency or another contributing process should be excluded. Despite the degree of anemia, he is currently clinically stable without reported cardiopulmonary symptoms.

Plan
Obtain CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers. Review for occult blood loss, nutritional factors, and other contributors to iron-restricted erythropoiesis. Coordinate management with the clinician overseeing his heart failure and consider hematology follow-up if anemia persists, worsens, or the evaluation is unrevealing. Iron replacement or erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy should be considered only after clarification of the underlying cause and assessment of renal and inflammatory status. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Seek urgent evaluation for new or worsening dyspnea, chest pain, syncope, palpitations, or marked weakness.
