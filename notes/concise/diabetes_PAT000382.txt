Chief Complaint

Medication review for type 2 diabetes.

Subjective

Morgan Oliver is a 67-year-old woman with 9-year history of type 2 diabetes, obesity, depression, and anxiety. Diabetes is not currently being actively managed pharmacologically. No diabetes medication adherence or tolerance concerns can be assessed today because no active regimen is reported. Denies polydipsia, polyuria, polyphagia, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. Smokes. Depression has been present for 11 years, anxiety for 8 years, and obesity for 38 years.

Objective

Height 162 cm; weight 100.3 kg; BMI 38.2 kg/m². BP 130/77 mmHg, pulse 76 bpm, respirations 15/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%. HbA1c 9.9%; fasting glucose 265 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Currently without reported hyperglycemic symptoms. Severe obesity and active smoking increase cardiometabolic risk. Depression and anxiety are relevant comorbidities.

Plan

Recommend prompt initiation of glucose-lowering therapy after medication reconciliation and assessment of renal function; metformin would be a reasonable first-line option if no contraindication. Discussed adherence, anticipated benefits, gastrointestinal and other potential adverse effects, and the importance of taking medication consistently. Counsel on reduced refined carbohydrate intake, portion control, gradual physical activity as tolerated, and weight reduction. Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, and routine diabetes complication screening. Encourage smoking cessation. Continue monitoring mood and anxiety, as these may affect self-management. Follow up soon for treatment initiation and repeat glycemic assessment.
