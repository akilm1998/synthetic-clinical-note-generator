Chief Complaint

Annual review and follow-up of chronic anemia.

Subjective

Deborah Lawson is a 56-year-old woman, 150 cm and 61.9 kg (BMI 27.5 kg/m²), presenting for comprehensive annual review. Anemia has been present for approximately 14 years and is currently not actively managed. She reports headache but denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, shortness of breath, palpitations, reduced exercise tolerance, or awareness of pallor.

Relevant chronic conditions include heart failure for 6 years and type 2 diabetes mellitus for 20 years. She currently smokes.

Objective

Blood pressure 121/80 mmHg, pulse 92 bpm, respiratory rate 24/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Recent laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.1 g/dL, hematocrit 24.1%, RBC 2.71 million/µL, MCV 89 fL, serum iron 23 µg/dL, ferritin 220 ng/mL, TIBC 201 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 11.4%.

Assessment

Anemia of chronic disease/inflammation, longstanding and currently untreated. Significant normocytic anemia with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and low TIBC, consistent with anemia associated with chronic disease. Headache may be related to anemia. No cardiopulmonary anemia symptoms reported, though respiratory rate is elevated at 24/min. Coexisting heart failure and diabetes may contribute to chronic disease burden.

Heart failure, 6-year history. No acute cardiopulmonary symptoms reported today; current treatment details were not provided.

Type 2 diabetes mellitus, 20-year history. Requires ongoing chronic disease monitoring.

Tobacco use.

Overweight by BMI.

Plan

Discussed laboratory findings and need for timely reassessment of the significant anemia. Recommend repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, renal function testing, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers. Review for chronic blood loss and other causes of anemia; consider hematology evaluation if persistent or unexplained. Iron replacement should be guided by the underlying cause and clinical evaluation rather than ferritin alone.

Advised urgent evaluation for new or worsening shortness of breath, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, syncope, marked weakness, or bleeding.

Continue routine follow-up of heart failure and diabetes with medication and monitoring review at the next visit. Encourage smoking cessation and provide preventive counseling, including age-appropriate cancer screening, immunizations, nutrition, physical activity as tolerated, and cardiovascular risk reduction. Follow up promptly after repeat anemia evaluation.
