Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Naomi Myers is a 50-year-old woman with a 9-year history of anemia, rheumatoid arthritis for 16 years, heart failure for 2 years, and type 2 diabetes mellitus for 1 year. She reports headache but denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, dyspnea, palpitations, reduced exercise tolerance, or awareness of pallor. Anemia has not been actively managed. She currently smokes. Height 162 cm, weight 70.3 kg, BMI 26.8 kg/m².

Objective
Blood pressure 120/81 mmHg, pulse 95 bpm, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Hemoglobin 9.1 g/dL, hematocrit 26.4%, RBC 3.11 million/µL, MCV 85 fL. Serum iron 38 µg/dL, ferritin 361 ng/mL, TIBC 201 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 18.9%.

Assessment
Anemia consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation, with normocytic indices, low serum iron and TIBC, and elevated ferritin. Chronic rheumatoid arthritis is the principal inflammatory context; heart failure and diabetes may also contribute. She is hemodynamically stable without cardiopulmonary symptoms, although headache persists and pulse is borderline elevated.

Plan
Obtain repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, along with vitamin B12, folate, renal function, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia.

Review for occult blood loss and other contributors if anemia worsens or laboratory findings are not concordant. Optimize management of underlying rheumatoid arthritis, heart failure, and diabetes in coordination with the appropriate treating clinicians.

Discuss iron replacement strategy only after reassessing iron availability and renal/inflammatory status; consider specialist input if anemia remains persistent or symptomatic. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Provide return precautions for new dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, or worsening weakness.
