Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective
Pamela Donovan is an 85-year-old woman with an 8-year history of type 2 diabetes, currently without reported complications. Diabetes has not been actively managed. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. No current diabetes medications are provided. She continues to smoke. History is also notable for longstanding non-alcoholic fatty liver disease.

Objective
Height 177 cm, weight 78.3 kg, BMI 25.0 kg/m². Blood pressure 131/81 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respirations 17/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 97%. HbA1c 9.1%; fasting glucose 211 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications, currently poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Patient is asymptomatic. Longstanding non-alcoholic fatty liver disease. Tobacco use.

Plan
Discussed elevated glucose and HbA1c and the importance of resuming active diabetes management. Recommend individualized treatment initiation or adjustment after reviewing current medication history, renal function, comorbidities, and treatment preferences, with attention to safety in advanced age. Encourage reduced refined carbohydrate intake, regular appropriate physical activity, and avoidance of hypoglycemia. Recommend assessment of renal function, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, and fasting lipid profile as part of diabetes care. Encourage smoking cessation. Follow up after treatment planning and repeat glycemic assessment.
