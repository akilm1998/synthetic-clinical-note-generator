Chief Complaint
Medication review for hypertension.

Subjective
Charles Bennett is a 79-year-old male with hypertension reported for 48 duration and coronary artery disease for 5 years. He is not currently on documented antihypertensive management. Denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, or epistaxis. Medication adherence and tolerance cannot be assessed because no current regimen is documented. Nonsmoker.

Objective
BP 151/88 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respirations 20/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 96%. Height 188 cm, weight 78.1 kg, BMI 22.1 kg/m². Serum sodium 144 mmol/L; potassium 3.9 mmol/L.

Assessment
Essential hypertension with elevated office blood pressure and no current documented treatment. No symptoms concerning for hypertensive emergency. Coronary artery disease noted.

Plan
Discussed importance of blood pressure control, particularly given age and coronary artery disease. Review and reconcile all current medications at the next contact; assess adherence and tolerance once a regimen is confirmed. Recommend initiation or adjustment of antihypertensive therapy through shared decision-making, with cautious titration and monitoring for dizziness or hypotension. Encourage home blood pressure monitoring and low-sodium diet. Consider ECG, lipid profile, urinalysis, and repeat serum electrolytes as part of cardiovascular risk and treatment assessment. Follow up promptly for blood pressure reassessment; seek urgent care for chest pain, shortness of breath, neurologic symptoms, or severe headache.
