Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Brooklyn Barnes is a 41-year-old woman presenting for follow-up of anemia present for approximately 13 years. She reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, shortness of breath, palpitations, reduced exercise tolerance, or noticeable pallor. She does not smoke. History is notable for malignancy diagnosed approximately 1 year ago. Anemia has not been actively managed.

Objective
Height 169 cm, weight 72.8 kg, BMI 25.5 kg/m². BP 116/79 mmHg, pulse 81 bpm, respirations 19/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Recent laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.2 g/dL, hematocrit 24.6%, RBC 2.89 million/µL, MCV 85 fL, serum iron 37 µg/dL, ferritin 308 ng/mL, TIBC 239 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 15.5%.

Assessment
Anemia of chronic disease/inflammation with longstanding, significant normocytic anemia. Iron studies show low serum iron and transferrin saturation with elevated ferritin and low TIBC, consistent with inflammatory iron restriction. Malignancy may be contributing. Patient is currently asymptomatic and hemodynamically stable.

Plan
Obtain updated CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, along with vitamin B12, folate, renal function, and inflammatory markers. Review anemia in the context of ongoing malignancy and coordinate evaluation with the treating oncology team. Assess for additional contributors, including occult blood loss, as clinically indicated. Discuss iron replacement or erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy only after review of the underlying cause and repeat laboratory assessment. Provide precautions to seek urgent care for new shortness of breath, chest pain, syncope, palpitations, or worsening weakness. Close outpatient follow-up after results.
