Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for elevated blood glucose.

Subjective
Henry Walsh is a 31-year-old man with approximately 10 years of type 2 diabetes, currently without active treatment documented. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. Smokes cigarettes. Past history includes chronic kidney disease for 1 year and anxiety for 2 years.

Objective
Height 179 cm; weight 91.6 kg; BMI 28.6 kg/m². BP 131/83 mmHg, pulse 76 bpm, respirations 15/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. HbA1c 9.7%; fasting glucose 222 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, poorly controlled based on HbA1c and fasting glucose. No current symptoms suggesting acute hyperglycemic illness. Chronic kidney disease and anxiety noted. Tobacco use.

Plan
Discussed significantly elevated glucose and need for prompt treatment and close follow-up. Review renal function before selecting diabetes medication; initiate appropriate glucose-lowering therapy at follow-up or sooner based on renal status. Recommend home glucose monitoring, diabetic nutrition counseling, regular physical activity, and weight reduction. Obtain/review serum creatinine/eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio; consider fasting lipid profile and routine diabetes eye and foot evaluation. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Reviewed emergency precautions for vomiting, abdominal pain, confusion, severe weakness, inability to maintain oral intake, or markedly elevated glucose readings. Close outpatient follow-up arranged.
