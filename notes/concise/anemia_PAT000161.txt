Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective

Ian Lawson is a 34-year-old man with a 13-year history of anemia, currently not under active management. He reports no fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, dyspnea, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. Pallor is noted. Relevant comorbidities include inflammatory bowel disease for 2 years, chronic infection for 4 years, and malignancy for 1 year. He does not smoke.

Objective

Height 181 cm, weight 72.4 kg, BMI 22.1 kg/m². Blood pressure 119/80 mmHg, pulse 83 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 99% on room air. Pallor present.

Laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.7 g/dL, hematocrit 26.9%, RBC 3.24 million/µL, MCV 83 fL, serum iron 36 µg/dL, ferritin 329 ng/mL, TIBC 249 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 14.5%.

Assessment

Anemia consistent with chronic disease/inflammation: significant, normocytic anemia with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and reduced TIBC. The pattern supports functional iron restriction in the setting of chronic inflammatory disease, chronic infection, and malignancy. He is currently hemodynamically stable without cardiopulmonary symptoms, but the hemoglobin level warrants expedited evaluation and management. Pallor is present.

Plan

Arrange prompt hematology evaluation and coordinate assessment of inflammatory bowel disease, chronic infection, and malignancy as potential ongoing drivers of anemia. Obtain repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers. Assess for active gastrointestinal blood loss or inflammatory bowel disease activity as clinically indicated. Review iron replacement strategy after evaluation of the inflammatory burden and iron profile; consider specialist-directed intravenous iron or erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy if appropriate. Advise urgent care for new dyspnea, chest pain, syncope, palpitations, or marked weakness.
