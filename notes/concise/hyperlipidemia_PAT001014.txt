Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up of markedly elevated cholesterol.

Subjective
Sydney Fuller is a 96-year-old woman, BMI 20.5 kg/m², with a 27-year history of hyperlipidemia that has not been actively managed. She has known coronary artery disease for 21 years and chronic kidney disease for 6 years. Denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, dizziness, or other reported acute cardiovascular symptoms. Xanthelasma is present. She does not smoke.

Objective
BP 139/85 mmHg, pulse 71 bpm, respirations 20/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 97% on room air. Height 164 cm; weight 55.1 kg.

Recent lipid panel: total cholesterol 349 mg/dL, LDL-C 229 mg/dL, HDL-C 47 mg/dL, triglycerides 367 mg/dL.

Assessment
Severe untreated dyslipidemia with markedly elevated LDL-C and triglycerides in the setting of established coronary artery disease and chronic kidney disease. Xanthelasma supports longstanding lipid elevation. No current symptoms suggestive of an acute coronary syndrome or limb ischemia.

Plan
Discussed substantially increased cardiovascular risk and need for active lipid management. Recommend prompt shared decision-making regarding initiation of lipid-lowering therapy, taking age, coronary artery disease, kidney disease, goals of care, and medication tolerance into account. Recommend repeat fasting lipid panel and review of renal/hepatic function and potential secondary contributors before or at treatment initiation. Reinforce heart-healthy diet, avoidance of trans fats and excess refined carbohydrates, and activity as tolerated. Seek emergency care for new chest pain, dyspnea, syncope, or focal neurologic symptoms. Close outpatient follow-up after treatment decision and laboratory reassessment.
