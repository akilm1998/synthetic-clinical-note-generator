Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up for elevated blood pressure with dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue.

Subjective

Diana Prince is a 77-year-old woman with a 28-year history of essential hypertension that has not been actively managed. She reports dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue. Denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, or epistaxis. She does not smoke.

Relevant comorbidities include coronary artery disease for 10 years, chronic kidney disease for 29 years, and type 2 diabetes mellitus for 32 years.

Objective

BP 159/88 mmHg, pulse 78 bpm, respirations 14/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 97%. Height 153 cm, weight 50.3 kg, BMI 21.5 kg/m².

Serum electrolytes: sodium 141 mmol/L, potassium 4.0 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension, longstanding and currently above goal in the setting of coronary artery disease, chronic kidney disease, and diabetes mellitus. Associated dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue without current symptoms suggestive of hypertensive emergency.

Plan

Confirm blood pressure with repeat seated measurement and review home readings if available. Perform medication reconciliation and establish or intensify antihypertensive therapy with consideration of renal function, coronary artery disease, and diabetes. Obtain ECG for palpitations and assess for orthostatic contribution to dizziness. Recommend urinalysis, lipid profile, and renal function assessment as part of hypertension risk evaluation.

Counseled on low-sodium diet, regular activity as tolerated, and prompt evaluation for chest pain, dyspnea, severe headache, focal neurologic symptoms, or markedly worsening blood pressure. Close outpatient follow-up after treatment plan is established.
