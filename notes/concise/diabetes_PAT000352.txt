Chief Complaint
Annual review of type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective
Nora Kelly is a 77-year-old woman with a 20-year history of type 2 diabetes and longstanding obesity. She is a nonsmoker. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. Reports numbness and tingling in the hands and feet. No current diabetes treatment regimen is documented.

Objective
Height 171 cm; weight 93.6 kg; BMI 32.0 kg/m². Blood pressure 140/78 mmHg, pulse 74 bpm, respiratory rate 19/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 96% on room air.
HbA1c 9.7%. Fasting glucose 227 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently markedly above glycemic target based on HbA1c and fasting glucose. Chronic paresthesias of the hands and feet warrant evaluation for diabetic peripheral nerve involvement. Obesity, BMI 32.0 kg/m². Borderline elevated office blood pressure.

Plan
Discussed need for active diabetes management and individualized glycemic targets considering age and comorbidity profile. Recommend initiation or intensification of glucose-lowering therapy after medication reconciliation and review of renal function; reinforce adherence once regimen is established.
Counseled on reducing refined carbohydrates, portion control, weight reduction, and safe regular physical activity.
Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, and repeat HbA1c for treatment planning and monitoring. Perform comprehensive diabetic foot examination, including sensory assessment and pulse evaluation, at follow-up; reinforce daily foot inspection and appropriate footwear.
Recommend routine dilated retinal examination and preventive care review, including age-appropriate immunizations. Follow up promptly after baseline evaluation and treatment initiation, with ongoing diabetes review within approximately 3 months.
