Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for hyperlipidemia.

Subjective
Valeria Ellis is a 48-year-old woman with a 7-year history of hyperlipidemia, currently not actively managed. She denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, or dizziness. Reports fatty skin deposits consistent with xanthomas; no xanthelasma reported. She continues to smoke. Relevant comorbidities include hypertension for 14 years, type 2 diabetes mellitus for 10 years, and chronic kidney disease for 1 year.

Objective
Height 169 cm, weight 73.4 kg, BMI 25.7 kg/m². BP 146/90 mmHg, pulse 77 bpm, respirations 16/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 96% on room air.

Lipid panel: total cholesterol 289 mg/dL, LDL-C 195 mg/dL, HDL-C 40 mg/dL, triglycerides 272 mg/dL. Xanthomas reported.

Assessment
Hyperlipidemia with markedly elevated LDL-C and elevated triglycerides, currently untreated. Increased cardiovascular risk in the setting of diabetes, hypertension, chronic kidney disease, tobacco use, and low HDL. Xanthomas raise concern for longstanding severe dyslipidemia.

Plan
Discussed cardiovascular risk reduction, heart-healthy diet, reduction of saturated and trans fats, regular physical activity as tolerated, weight maintenance, and smoking cessation.

Recommend initiation of high-intensity statin therapy after medication reconciliation and shared decision-making. Consider additional lipid risk assessment with ApoB and lipoprotein(a), and evaluate for secondary contributors as clinically appropriate. Repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation to assess response.

Continue close management of blood pressure, diabetes, and kidney disease with the primary care team. Follow up after medication initiation and repeat lipid testing.
