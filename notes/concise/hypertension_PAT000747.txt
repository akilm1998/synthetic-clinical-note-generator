Chief Complaint

Annual review and hypertension follow-up.

Subjective

Edward Holland is a 38-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. He has a 4-year history of essential hypertension that has not been actively managed. Reports intermittent dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue. Denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, or epistaxis. Current smoking status is positive. Known coronary artery disease for approximately 1 year and hyperlipidemia for 8 years. Weight 99.7 kg; BMI 28.2 kg/m².

Objective

Vital signs: BP 140/93 mmHg, pulse 80 bpm, respirations 17/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Height 188 cm.  
Serum electrolytes: sodium 146 mmol/L, potassium 3.8 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension with above-goal office blood pressure and associated intermittent dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue; currently without active management. No symptoms suggesting acute hypertensive complication today.

Coronary artery disease, duration approximately 1 year.

Hyperlipidemia, duration approximately 8 years.

Tobacco use.

Overweight by BMI.

Plan

Confirm blood pressure burden with repeat office measurement and home monitoring; review readings at close follow-up. Discuss initiation of antihypertensive therapy, particularly given coexisting coronary artery disease, after medication reconciliation and cardiovascular risk review.

Recommend ECG for palpitations and known coronary artery disease. Obtain lipid profile and urinalysis as part of hypertension and cardiovascular risk assessment. Monitor renal function and electrolytes if antihypertensive therapy is initiated.

Counseled on smoking cessation, reduced sodium intake, heart-healthy diet, regular physical activity, and weight reduction.

Review preventive care, including immunizations and age-appropriate cardiovascular and general health screening.

Follow up promptly for chest pain, dyspnea, neurologic symptoms, severe headache, or markedly elevated home blood pressure. Return for blood pressure and chronic disease review after home readings are obtained.
