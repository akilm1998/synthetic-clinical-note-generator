Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective
Ariana Lawson is a 42-year-old woman with a 15-year history of anemia, inflammatory bowel disease for 14 years, and rheumatoid arthritis for 4 years. She is not currently receiving documented anemia-directed management. She reports headache and pallor but denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, dyspnea, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. She does not smoke.

Objective
Height 174 cm, weight 83.6 kg, BMI 27.6 kg/m². Blood pressure 123/79 mmHg, pulse 81 bpm, respiratory rate 22/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 97%. Pallor noted. Cardiovascular and respiratory examinations are otherwise documented as normal.

Hemoglobin 8.5 g/dL, hematocrit 24.9%, RBC 3.0 million/µL, MCV 83 fL. Serum iron 30 µg/dL, ferritin 182 ng/mL, TIBC 198 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 15.2%.

Assessment
Chronic normocytic anemia consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation, in the setting of longstanding inflammatory bowel disease and rheumatoid arthritis. Iron-restricted profile with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated/maintained ferritin, and low TIBC. Hemoglobin is significantly reduced, although she is currently hemodynamically stable and minimally symptomatic aside from headache and pallor.

Plan
Obtain/update CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia. Review current inflammatory bowel disease and rheumatoid arthritis activity and coordinate optimization of underlying inflammatory disease management. Recommend hematology and/or gastroenterology/rheumatology follow-up for consideration of iron replacement strategy, including intravenous iron if clinically appropriate, and assessment for erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy only if indicated by the underlying disease and renal status. Advise urgent evaluation for new dyspnea, chest pain, syncope, palpitations, or worsening bleeding.
