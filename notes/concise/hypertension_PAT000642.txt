Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Subjective

Violet Murphy is a 44-year-old woman with a 13-year history of hypertension, currently not actively managed. She reports intermittent palpitations and fatigue. Denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, or nosebleeds. She also has hyperlipidemia for approximately 5 years and chronic kidney disease for 3 years. She smokes.

Objective

BP 154/96 mmHg, pulse 80 bpm, respirations 18/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 99%. Height 168 cm, weight 66.6 kg, BMI 23.6 kg/m². Serum sodium 139 mmol/L and potassium 4.6 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension, persistently above goal and currently untreated/not actively managed. No symptoms concerning for hypertensive emergency today. Palpitations and fatigue noted without associated chest pain, dyspnea, or dizziness.

Chronic kidney disease and hyperlipidemia, established comorbidities increasing cardiovascular risk.

Tobacco use.

Plan

Discussed importance of consistent blood pressure management, low-sodium diet, regular physical activity, and home blood pressure monitoring. Recommend initiation of antihypertensive therapy with selection guided by chronic kidney disease and repeat blood pressure readings. Review home readings at close follow-up.

Recommend ECG for evaluation of palpitations and urinalysis, lipid profile, and renal function/electrolyte monitoring as part of hypertension and kidney disease assessment. Counsel on smoking cessation and cardiovascular risk reduction.

Follow up soon for blood pressure reassessment; seek urgent care for chest pain, shortness of breath, severe headache, neurologic symptoms, or markedly elevated blood pressure.
