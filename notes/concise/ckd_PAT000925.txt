Chief Complaint

Annual review of chronic kidney disease and hypertension.

Subjective

Hailey Johnston is a 46-year-old woman presenting for annual review. She has a 16-year history of chronic kidney disease and a 4-year history of hypertension. She denies fatigue, pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, sleep disturbance, peripheral edema, change in urination, and shortness of breath. She currently smokes. No active CKD-directed treatment regimen is documented.

Objective

Height 154 cm, weight 61.9 kg, BMI 26.1 kg/m². Blood pressure 146/91 mmHg, pulse 79/min, respiratory rate 14/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Serum creatinine 6.0 mg/dL, BUN 74 mg/dL, eGFR 5 mL/min/1.73 m².

Assessment

Chronic kidney disease with markedly reduced renal function, currently asymptomatic but with significant biochemical impairment. Hypertension remains above goal and is an important modifiable risk factor. The absence of uremic or fluid-overload symptoms does not lessen the need for prompt specialist assessment. Tobacco use and overweight status also increase cardiovascular and renal risk.

Plan

Arrange urgent nephrology evaluation for comprehensive advanced kidney disease management and discussion of renal replacement therapy planning, as clinically appropriate. Obtain urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum electrolytes, CBC, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound as part of renal assessment. Review blood pressure management and home readings with attention to renal-protective therapy; medication reconciliation is required before treatment decisions. Counsel on smoking cessation, sodium restriction, avoidance of NSAIDs and other nephrotoxins, and maintenance of hydration appropriate to renal status. Review immunizations and age-appropriate preventive care, including cardiovascular risk reduction. Seek urgent care for new shortness of breath, swelling, markedly reduced urine output, persistent nausea or vomiting, confusion, or chest pain. Close follow-up is required after nephrology assessment.
