Chief Complaint
Diabetes follow-up.

Subjective
Jane Watson is a 62-year-old woman with an 8-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, currently not actively managed. She is not taking diabetes medication or checking glucose regularly. Denies polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia, unexplained weight loss, blurred vision, chest pain, dyspnea, or foot ulcers. Reports intermittent fatigue. No numbness or tingling of the feet. History of hypertension for 27 years and anxiety. Never smoker.

Objective
BP 148/86 mmHg, pulse 78 bpm, respirations 16/min, temperature 98.2°F, SpO2 98% on room air. BMI 31.2 kg/m².

Alert and oriented, no acute distress. Cardiopulmonary examination normal. Abdomen soft and nontender. Feet warm with intact skin, palpable dorsalis pedis pulses, and intact monofilament sensation bilaterally. No edema or ulcers.

Hemoglobin A1c 8.4%. Fasting glucose 176 mg/dL. Creatinine 0.9 mg/dL, eGFR 72 mL/min/1.73 m². Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio 18 mg/g. LDL 112 mg/dL. 

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications, currently above goal and not managed.
Hypertension, suboptimally controlled.
Anxiety, chronic.

Plan
Start metformin 500 mg once daily with food; increase to 500 mg twice daily as tolerated. Discussed dietary carbohydrate reduction, weight loss, regular physical activity, and home glucose monitoring. Reviewed hypoglycemia precautions. Refer for diabetic education and nutrition counseling.

Obtain diabetic eye examination. Continue routine foot care and daily inspection. Repeat hemoglobin A1c, metabolic panel, and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio in 3 months.

Continue current antihypertensive regimen and begin home blood pressure monitoring; bring readings to follow-up. Return in 4 weeks for medication and blood pressure review, or sooner for hyperglycemia symptoms or medication intolerance.
