Chief Complaint

Annual review and chronic disease follow-up.

Subjective

Gavin Franklin is a 93-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. Chronic kidney disease has been present for approximately 47 years and is currently not under active management. He denies fatigue, pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, peripheral edema, change in urination, sleep disturbance, or shortness of breath. History also includes type 2 diabetes mellitus for 38 years, hyperlipidemia for 37 years, and gout for 9 years. He currently smokes.

Objective

Height 163 cm; weight 71.5 kg; BMI 26.9 kg/m². Blood pressure 147/86 mmHg, pulse 74 bpm, respiratory rate 15/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 96%.

Recent laboratory results: creatinine 4.13 mg/dL, eGFR 15 mL/min/1.73 m², BUN 91 mg/dL.

Assessment

1. Chronic kidney disease with markedly reduced renal function and elevated BUN/creatinine. He is presently without reported uremic or volume-overload symptoms. Blood pressure remains above goal. No current medication regimen is documented.

2. Type 2 diabetes mellitus, longstanding. Glycemic control data are not available for review today.

3. Hyperlipidemia, longstanding.

4. Gout, chronic history.

5. Tobacco use.

6. Preventive health maintenance in a 93-year-old man.

Plan

Recommend prompt nephrology evaluation and renewed chronic kidney disease management. Repeat renal function and serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, and intact parathyroid hormone as clinically appropriate; consider renal ultrasound. Review renal-dose medication safety and avoid NSAIDs and other nephrotoxins. Discuss warning symptoms requiring urgent assessment, including new shortness of breath, swelling, persistent nausea or vomiting, confusion, or significant change in urination.

Reassess blood pressure and diabetes management, including medication reconciliation and appropriate monitoring. Review cardiovascular risk management and gout treatment with attention to renal function.

Counseled regarding smoking cessation. Review age-appropriate immunizations, fall-risk assessment, vision and hearing care, dental care, and individualized cancer screening and advance care planning. Arrange close primary care follow-up after renal evaluation.
