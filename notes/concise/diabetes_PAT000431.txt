Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes.

Subjective

Jason Coleman is a 45-year-old man, 188 cm and 88.0 kg (BMI 24.9 kg/m²), with an 8-year history of type 2 diabetes, hypertension for 11 years, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease for 3 years, and obstructive sleep apnea for 5 years. Diabetes has not been actively managed. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the hands or feet. He continues to smoke.

Objective

Blood pressure 139/86 mmHg, pulse 76 bpm, respirations 14/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98%. Hemoglobin A1c 9.4%. Fasting glucose 235 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently poorly controlled based on elevated A1c and fasting glucose. No hyperglycemic, neuropathic, or infectious symptoms reported.

Hypertension with borderline elevated office blood pressure.

Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease.

Obstructive sleep apnea.

Active tobacco use.

Plan

Discussed significance of current hyperglycemia and risks of untreated diabetes. Recommend initiating an appropriate diabetes treatment regimen and establishing regular glucose monitoring; medication selection and adherence reviewed. Counsel on carbohydrate-conscious nutrition, regular physical activity, and maintaining current healthy weight. Recommend repeat HbA1c and fasting glucose after treatment is established, along with urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, and routine diabetic eye and foot assessments.

Continue monitoring blood pressure and reinforce sodium reduction, exercise, and tobacco cessation. Encourage adherence to sleep apnea management. Continue lifestyle measures for fatty liver disease and avoid excess alcohol. Follow up promptly to reassess diabetes control and treatment response.
