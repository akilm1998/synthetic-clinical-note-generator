Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Ellie Wood is a 98-year-old woman presenting for follow-up of anemia present for approximately 58 months. She reports no fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, pallor, shortness of breath, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. She is a nonsmoker. Comorbidity includes malignancy diagnosed approximately 2 years ago. No current anemia treatment is documented.

Objective
Height 165 cm; weight 79.2 kg; BMI 29.1 kg/m². BP 133/77 mmHg, pulse 80 bpm, respirations 19/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Hemoglobin 8.5 g/dL, hematocrit 26.1%, RBC 3.14 million/µL, MCV 83 fL. Serum iron 49 µg/dL, ferritin 192 ng/mL, TIBC 216 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 22.7%.

Assessment
Anemia consistent with chronic disease/inflammation: significant, normocytic anemia with low serum iron and TIBC and preserved ferritin. Currently asymptomatic and hemodynamically stable. Malignancy may be contributing.

Plan
Repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear; consider renal function, inflammatory markers, vitamin B12, and folate testing to further evaluate persistent anemia. Review for occult blood loss and nutritional contributors as clinically indicated. Coordinate management with the clinician overseeing her malignancy. Consider hematology evaluation and iron or erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy only after assessment of the underlying cause and treatment goals. Seek urgent care for new dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, bleeding, or marked weakness.
