Chief Complaint

Annual review and chronic disease follow-up.

Subjective

Sarah Griffin is a 96-year-old woman presenting for comprehensive annual review. She has a 50-year history of hyperlipidemia, currently without documented lipid-lowering treatment, and coronary artery disease diagnosed 8 years ago. Recent lipid panel shows markedly elevated cholesterol and LDL with elevated triglycerides. She denies chest discomfort, intermittent claudication, or dizziness. Xanthomas and xanthelasma are present. She does not smoke.

Objective

Height 165 cm; weight 80.0 kg; BMI 29.4 kg/m². Blood pressure 128/75 mmHg, pulse 71 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 95%.

Laboratory results: total cholesterol 315 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 207 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 50 mg/dL, triglycerides 289 mg/dL.

Assessment

1. Hyperlipidemia, longstanding and currently inadequately controlled, with severe LDL elevation and moderate hypertriglyceridemia. Xanthomas and xanthelasma are consistent with prolonged lipid elevation. No current anginal or claudication symptoms.

2. Coronary artery disease, known for 8 years, without reported current chest discomfort or dizziness. Lipid control is particularly important for secondary cardiovascular risk reduction.

3. BMI 29.4 kg/m².

Plan

Discuss initiation of lipid-lowering therapy, with treatment intensity and tolerability individualized to her age, preferences, comorbid coronary artery disease, and goals of care. Review prior medication tolerance if available and consider a statin-based regimen; additional therapy may be considered if LDL remains substantially elevated.

Recommend heart-healthy dietary changes emphasizing reduced saturated and trans fats, limiting refined carbohydrates, and regular activity appropriate to functional status. Repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment changes to assess response; consider evaluation for secondary contributors if clinically indicated.

Continue monitoring for chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, or new dizziness and seek prompt evaluation if these develop. Review preventive care, immunizations, fall risk, functional status, and advance care preferences as part of ongoing annual care.
