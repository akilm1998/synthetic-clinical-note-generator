Chief Complaint

New patient consultation for longstanding hyperlipidemia.

Subjective

Maya Russell is an 86-year-old woman with a 27-year history of untreated hyperlipidemia, coronary artery disease for approximately 19 years, chronic kidney disease for 25 years, and non-alcoholic fatty liver disease for 3 years. She does not smoke. She denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, dizziness, xanthomas, or xanthelasma. No current lipid-lowering treatment is documented.

Recent lipid profile shows total cholesterol 326 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 229 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 35 mg/dL, and triglycerides 312 mg/dL.

Objective

Height 153 cm; weight 47.5 kg; BMI 20.3 kg/m². Blood pressure 145/83 mmHg, pulse 79 bpm, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 95%. No additional examination findings documented.

Assessment

Severe longstanding mixed dyslipidemia with markedly elevated LDL cholesterol and elevated triglycerides, currently untreated. Her coronary artery disease places her at very high cardiovascular risk despite absence of active ischemic symptoms. Management requires consideration of advanced age, chronic kidney disease, and non-alcoholic fatty liver disease. Mildly elevated blood pressure also noted.

Plan

Discussed the importance of lipid-lowering therapy for secondary cardiovascular prevention. Recommend shared decision-making regarding initiation of a statin, with cautious dosing and adjustment for tolerance, renal function, and overall goals of care. If LDL reduction is inadequate or statin therapy is not tolerated, consider addition of ezetimibe; specialist-directed therapy may be considered if needed.

Recommend heart-healthy diet emphasizing reduced saturated and trans fats, limitation of refined carbohydrates and alcohol, and continuation of activity as safely tolerated. Review renal and hepatic function before and during therapy. Recommend repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation or adjustment, with monitoring for muscle symptoms or other adverse effects. Continue assessment for ischemic symptoms and coordinate management of her established coronary artery disease and chronic kidney disease.
