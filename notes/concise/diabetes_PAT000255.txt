Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes.

Subjective
Aaron Price is a 37-year-old man with a 5-year history of type 2 diabetes and 2-year history of hypertension. Diabetes has not been actively managed. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. Reports numbness in the hands or feet without tingling. He does not smoke.

Objective
Height 162 cm, weight 76.6 kg, BMI 29.2 kg/m². BP 133/86 mmHg, pulse 68 bpm, respirations 16/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 97% on room air. HbA1c 9.7%; fasting glucose 234 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Distal numbness is concerning for possible early diabetic neuropathic symptoms. Overweight. Hypertension with acceptable office blood pressure today.

Plan
Discussed the importance of active diabetes treatment, nutrition changes, weight reduction, and regular physical activity. Recommend initiating evidence-based glucose-lowering therapy after shared decision-making, with close follow-up to assess response. Recommend home glucose monitoring as appropriate and review of hypoglycemia precautions if medication is started. Arrange diabetic foot examination, retinal screening, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, renal function, and fasting lipid assessment. Counsel on daily foot care and prompt evaluation of wounds or progressive sensory changes. Continue blood pressure monitoring and follow up in approximately 4–6 weeks for diabetes treatment adjustment and review of glucose readings.
