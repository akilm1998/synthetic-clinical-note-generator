Chief Complaint

Initial consultation for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Richard Lawson is a 32-year-old man presenting for initial outpatient evaluation of type 2 diabetes diagnosed approximately 14 years ago. Diabetes is currently not actively managed. Recent HbA1c is 9.7% and fasting glucose is 215 mg/dL. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, blurred vision, fatigue, unintentional weight loss, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the extremities.

History is notable for obesity for approximately 13 years and hypertension diagnosed approximately 1 year ago. He does not smoke. No current diabetes medications were provided.

Objective

Vital signs: BP 138/86 mmHg, pulse 79 bpm, respirations 17/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Height 167 cm, weight 86.5 kg, BMI 31.0 kg/m².

Laboratory results: HbA1c 9.7%; fasting glucose 215 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently significantly above glycemic goal based on HbA1c and fasting glucose. Patient denies symptomatic hyperglycemia, neuropathic symptoms, visual symptoms, or infection-related concerns.

Obesity, BMI 31.0 kg/m².

Hypertension, with mildly elevated office blood pressure today.

Plan

Discussed abnormal glycemic results and risks of untreated hyperglycemia. Recommend initiation of active diabetes treatment and close outpatient follow-up; medication options and risks/benefits to be reviewed with the patient.

Counseled regarding reduction of concentrated carbohydrates and sugary beverages, portion control, weight reduction, and gradual increase in regular physical activity as tolerated.

Recommend home glucose monitoring and keeping a log for review.

Obtain renal function, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, and fasting lipid profile as part of diabetes risk assessment. Recommend comprehensive foot examination and dilated retinal examination. Continue monitoring blood pressure and address cardiovascular risk factors.

Follow up promptly to establish the treatment regimen and reassess glucose control.
