Assessment
Hyperlipidemia, longstanding and currently untreated, with severe LDL elevation and moderate hypertriglyceridemia. Xanthomas and xanthelasma support significant chronic lipid burden. No current symptoms suggestive of angina or claudication.
Hypertension, with blood pressure above goal today.

Plan
Discussed markedly abnormal lipid levels and increased long-term cardiovascular risk. Recommend initiation of high-intensity statin therapy after shared decision-making, with consideration of additional lipid-lowering therapy if response is inadequate or statin intolerance occurs. Review for secondary contributors and possible inherited lipid disorder; recommend repeat fasting lipid panel and consideration of ApoB and lipoprotein(a) testing. Counsel on a heart-healthy diet low in saturated and trans fats, regular aerobic activity, and continued avoidance of tobacco. Reassess blood pressure and overall cardiovascular risk at follow-up. Return after treatment initiation and repeat lipid assessment.
