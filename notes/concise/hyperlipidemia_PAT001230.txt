Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for hyperlipidemia.

Subjective
Sean Palmer is a 36-year-old man with a 7-year history of hyperlipidemia, currently not receiving documented treatment. He denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, dizziness, xanthomas, and xanthelasma. He continues to smoke. Comorbid hypertension for 4 years and type 2 diabetes mellitus for 5 years.

Objective
Height 173 cm; weight 67.0 kg; BMI 22.4 kg/m². BP 132/86 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respirations 18/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Recent lipid panel: total cholesterol 350 mg/dL, LDL-C 262 mg/dL, HDL-C 30 mg/dL, triglycerides 288 mg/dL.

Assessment
Hyperlipidemia, unspecified. Markedly elevated LDL-C with concomitant hypertriglyceridemia and low HDL-C. Longstanding untreated dyslipidemia in the setting of diabetes, hypertension, and active tobacco use confers substantially increased cardiovascular risk despite normal BMI. No current symptoms suggestive of angina or claudication.

Hypertension, 4-year history. Blood pressure mildly elevated today.

Type 2 diabetes mellitus, 5-year history. Requires continued risk-factor optimization.

Tobacco use. Ongoing smoking increases cardiovascular risk.

Plan
Discussed the significant cardiovascular risk associated with the current lipid profile and comorbid diabetes and tobacco use. Recommend initiation of high-intensity statin therapy after shared decision-making, with adherence and adverse effects reviewed. Consider additional lipid-lowering therapy if LDL-C remains substantially elevated despite treatment.

Recommend repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation, with consideration of ApoB, lipoprotein(a), and evaluation for secondary causes or inherited dyslipidemia given LDL-C of 262 mg/dL at a young age.

Counseled on a heart-healthy diet low in saturated and trans fats, regular aerobic activity, and smoking cessation. Continue blood pressure and diabetes management with emphasis on cardiovascular risk reduction. Follow up after repeat lipid testing to reassess response and treatment plan.
