Chief Complaint

Annual review and chronic kidney disease follow-up.

Subjective

Scott Douglas is a 33-year-old man, height 172 cm, weight 77.5 kg, BMI 26.2 kg/m², presenting for comprehensive annual review. Chronic kidney disease has been present for approximately 2 years and is currently not under active management. He denies fatigue, pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, peripheral edema, change in urination, sleep disturbance, and shortness of breath. Current comorbidities include secondary hyperparathyroidism for 1 year and hyperlipidemia for 2 years. He reports current tobacco use.

Objective

Vital signs: BP 136/90 mmHg, pulse 76 bpm, respirations 14/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Laboratory results: creatinine 3.03 mg/dL, eGFR 16 mL/min/1.73 m², BUN 95 mg/dL.

Assessment

Chronic kidney disease with significantly reduced renal function and marked azotemia. Despite absence of uremic or volume-overload symptoms, this requires prompt renal evaluation and active management. Blood pressure is mildly elevated. Secondary hyperparathyroidism and hyperlipidemia noted. Current smoking.

Plan

Arrange expedited nephrology referral for comprehensive CKD evaluation and management. Obtain repeat renal function and serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, intact PTH, and renal ultrasound as clinically indicated. Review renal dosing and avoidance of NSAIDs and other nephrotoxins; medication reconciliation is needed before initiating therapy. Monitor blood pressure and dietary sodium intake.

Discuss tobacco cessation and cardiovascular risk reduction, including regular physical activity and weight maintenance. Review immunizations and age-appropriate preventive screening at follow-up. Seek urgent care for new nausea or vomiting, worsening fatigue, confusion, reduced urine output, edema, or shortness of breath. Follow up promptly after nephrology assessment and laboratory reassessment.
