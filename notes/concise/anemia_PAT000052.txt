Chief Complaint
Annual review and chronic anemia assessment.

Subjective
Ellie Wood is a 78-year-old woman, height 150 cm, weight 45.9 kg, BMI 20.4 kg/m², presenting for comprehensive annual review. She has a 25-year history of anemia and is not currently under active anemia management. She denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, pallor, reduced exercise tolerance, dyspnea, palpitations, or other anemia-related symptoms.

Relevant chronic conditions include rheumatoid arthritis for 42 years, chronic infection for 16 years, and malignancy for 21 years. She continues to smoke.

Objective
Blood pressure 130/76 mmHg, pulse 84 bpm, respiratory rate 22/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 94%.

Hemoglobin 8.8 g/dL, hematocrit 27.2%, RBC 3.06 million/µL, MCV 89 fL. Serum iron 24 µg/dL, ferritin 383 ng/mL, TIBC 252 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 9.5%.

Assessment
Chronic, clinically asymptomatic normocytic anemia with reduced hemoglobin and hematocrit. The low serum iron and transferrin saturation with elevated ferritin and low TIBC are most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/chronic disease, in the context of longstanding rheumatoid arthritis, chronic infection, and malignancy. Iron-restricted erythropoiesis or an additional contributing process should be excluded. No current evidence of symptomatic cardiopulmonary compromise by history; respiratory rate is mildly increased and oxygen saturation is 94%.

Plan
Recommend updated anemia evaluation with CBC including indices, reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function, and inflammatory markers. Review for occult blood loss and nutritional factors as clinically indicated. Management should address the underlying inflammatory, infectious, and malignant conditions in coordination with the appropriate treating specialists. Consider hematology review given persistent hemoglobin 8.8 g/dL and 25-year duration; assess suitability for iron therapy only after clarification of iron status and contributing causes.

Annual preventive care reviewed, including smoking cessation counseling, immunization status, fall-risk assessment, nutritional review, and individualized age-appropriate cancer and cardiovascular screening. Return for review of anemia evaluation or earlier for dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, bleeding, or worsening functional status.
