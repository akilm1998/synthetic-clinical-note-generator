Chief Complaint

Initial consultation for chronic kidney disease.

Subjective

Philip Donovan is a 92-year-old man, 180 cm, 90.7 kg, BMI 28.0 kg/m², presenting for initial outpatient evaluation of chronic kidney disease diagnosed approximately 11 years ago. Current management has not been established. Reports fatigue. Denies pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, peripheral edema, change in urinary frequency, sleep disturbance, or shortness of breath. History also notable for anemia of chronic disease for approximately 33 years. He currently smokes. Medication and allergy information were not provided.

Objective

Vital signs: BP 152/87 mmHg, pulse 81/min, respirations 22/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 96% on room air.

Laboratory results: Creatinine 3.49 mg/dL, eGFR 19 mL/min/1.73 m², BUN 95 mg/dL.

No additional examination findings documented.

Assessment

Chronic kidney disease of approximately 11 years’ duration with markedly reduced renal function and substantially elevated BUN. Fatigue may be related to chronic kidney disease and longstanding anemia. No current symptoms of fluid overload or overt uremic syndrome reported. Elevated blood pressure today. Active tobacco use.

Plan

Arrange prompt nephrology consultation for comprehensive CKD evaluation and renal replacement therapy planning as clinically appropriate.

Obtain/review serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound as part of CKD assessment.

Review all current medications and avoid NSAIDs and other potentially nephrotoxic agents unless specifically directed. Assess blood pressure management and renal-protective treatment at follow-up.

Counsel regarding smoking cessation, avoidance of excess dietary sodium, and prompt reporting of reduced urine output, worsening fatigue, nausea or vomiting, confusion, new edema, chest pain, or shortness of breath. Given BUN 95 mg/dL and eGFR 19, seek urgent evaluation for any such symptoms.

Follow up promptly after nephrology evaluation and laboratory review.
