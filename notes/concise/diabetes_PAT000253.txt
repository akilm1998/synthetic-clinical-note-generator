Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Simone Bishop is a 63-year-old woman presenting for diabetes follow-up. She has an 18-year history of type 2 diabetes and is not currently receiving active diabetes management. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. No smoking history. Comorbidities include dyslipidemia for 23 years, anxiety for 41 years, and obesity for 2 years.

Objective

Height 159 cm; weight 82.7 kg; BMI 32.7 kg/m². BP 125/81 mmHg, pulse 74 bpm, respirations 22/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 97%. HbA1c 9.9%; fasting glucose 268 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Currently without reported hyperglycemic, neuropathic, infectious, or constitutional symptoms.

Obesity. Dyslipidemia. Anxiety.

Plan

Discussed abnormal glucose results and need to begin an active diabetes treatment plan. Review dietary pattern, carbohydrate intake, physical activity, and home glucose monitoring. Discuss initiation of appropriate glucose-lowering pharmacotherapy, with medication selection based on renal function, comorbidities, cost, and patient preference. Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, foot examination, and diabetic retinal screening as part of routine diabetes care. Encourage gradual weight reduction and regular physical activity as tolerated. Follow up after treatment initiation and glucose review.
