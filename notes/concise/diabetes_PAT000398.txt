Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for elevated diabetes labs.

Subjective
Cody Warren is a 31-year-old man with a 6-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, currently without reported management or active treatment plan. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. Nonsmoker. History also notable for depression for approximately 10 years.

Objective
Height 162 cm; weight 64.3 kg; BMI 24.5 kg/m². Blood pressure 125/79 mmHg, pulse 76 bpm, respirations 18/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%. HbA1c 9.6%; fasting glucose 235 mg/dL.

Assessment
1. Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications, significantly above glycemic goal based on HbA1c and fasting glucose. Despite biochemical hyperglycemia, he reports no osmotic or constitutional symptoms and has no reported neuropathic or infectious complications.

2. Depression, longstanding, approximately 10 years. No current psychiatric symptoms or treatment details provided.

Plan
Discussed that current glucose values require prompt outpatient treatment and closer monitoring. Arrange medication reconciliation and initiation or adjustment of guideline-directed diabetes therapy with shared decision-making; reinforce adherence once treatment is established. Counsel on reducing concentrated carbohydrates, regular physical activity as tolerated, and home glucose monitoring with instructions to report persistently severe elevations or development of polyuria, polydipsia, vomiting, abdominal pain, confusion, or weakness. Recommend diabetes complication screening, including renal assessment, lipid evaluation, and retinal and foot examinations. Reassess glycemic control after treatment initiation and address mood symptoms and safety at follow-up.
