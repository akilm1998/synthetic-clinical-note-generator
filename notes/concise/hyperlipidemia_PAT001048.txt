Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up of hyperlipidemia.

Subjective

Eliana Matthews is an 80-year-old woman presenting for follow-up of longstanding hyperlipidemia, present for approximately 43 years and currently untreated. She denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, dizziness, xanthomas, or xanthelasma. She does not smoke. History is also notable for non-alcoholic fatty liver disease for 3 years. No medication use was provided.

Objective

Height 152 cm; weight 48.3 kg; BMI 20.9 kg/m². Blood pressure 138/76 mmHg, pulse 68 bpm, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 95%.

Recent lipid panel:
Total cholesterol 378 mg/dL
LDL cholesterol 268 mg/dL
HDL cholesterol 46 mg/dL
Triglycerides 320 mg/dL

Assessment

Hyperlipidemia with marked elevation of LDL cholesterol and triglycerides, currently untreated. No reported cardiovascular or cutaneous symptoms. Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease is a relevant comorbidity.

Plan

Discussed significantly elevated cardiovascular risk associated with the lipid levels and potential benefits and risks of treatment at her age. Recommend shared decision-making regarding initiation of statin therapy, with consideration of a moderate- to high-intensity statin if no contraindication and patient is agreeable.

Counseled on a heart-healthy diet emphasizing reduction of saturated and trans fats, limiting refined carbohydrates, and maintaining regular physical activity as tolerated. Continue avoidance of tobacco.

Recommend repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation or lifestyle intervention to assess response. Consider evaluation for secondary contributors and baseline liver testing given history of fatty liver disease before or soon after starting therapy. Follow up in clinic to review treatment preferences, tolerance, and lipid response.
