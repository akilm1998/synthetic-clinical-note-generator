Chief Complaint

Initial outpatient consultation for chronic kidney disease.

Subjective

Sophie Bryant is a 98-year-old woman, 152 cm, 65.4 kg (BMI 28.3 kg/m²), presenting for initial evaluation of chronic kidney disease present for approximately 38 years. She reports no fatigue, pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, sleep disturbance, peripheral edema, change in urination, or shortness of breath. History is notable for hypertension diagnosed approximately 7 years ago. She does not smoke. No current medication regimen is documented.

Objective

Blood pressure 162/96 mmHg, pulse 69/min, respirations 15/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 97%.

Serum creatinine 5.61 mg/dL. BUN 62 mg/dL. eGFR 9 mL/min/1.73 m².

Assessment

Chronic kidney disease with markedly reduced renal function, currently without reported uremic or fluid-overload symptoms. Management has not been established. Elevated blood pressure in the setting of CKD.

Plan

Recommend urgent nephrology consultation for comprehensive CKD management and discussion of goals of care and renal replacement planning as clinically appropriate.

Obtain/consider repeat renal function testing and serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, renal ultrasound, and intact parathyroid hormone level.

Address blood pressure promptly after medication reconciliation and assessment of overall goals and tolerability.

Avoid NSAIDs and other potentially nephrotoxic agents; review all prescription, over-the-counter, and herbal medications with a clinician.

Seek emergency evaluation for new or worsening shortness of breath, chest pain, confusion, persistent vomiting, marked weakness, inability to urinate, or new swelling.

Close outpatient follow-up is recommended.
