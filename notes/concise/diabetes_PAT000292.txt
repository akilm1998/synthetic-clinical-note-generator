Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes.

Subjective
Emma Carter is an 86-year-old woman with a 35-year history of type 2 diabetes, currently without active diabetes management. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. Reports numbness in the hands or feet without tingling. She continues to smoke. Longstanding obesity is noted, present for approximately 56 years.

Objective
Height 154 cm; weight 94.4 kg; BMI 39.8 kg/m². BP 129/77 mmHg, pulse 84 bpm, respirations 21/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 95%. HbA1c 9.3%. Fasting glucose 206 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complication, currently above goal based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Distal numbness reported, requiring ongoing foot-risk assessment. Severe obesity. Tobacco use.

Plan
Discussed elevated glucose results and importance of active diabetes management. Recommend initiation of an individualized glucose-lowering treatment plan, taking age, comorbidity risk, and functional status into account. Encourage reduced refined carbohydrate intake, portion control, and activity as tolerated. Recommend home glucose monitoring if treatment is initiated. Reinforce daily foot inspection and prompt evaluation of wounds or infection. Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, renal function assessment, lipid profile, and diabetic eye and foot evaluations as part of routine care. Smoking cessation counseling provided. Follow up soon to review treatment options and glycemic monitoring.
