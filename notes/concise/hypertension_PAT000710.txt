Chief Complaint
Annual review of longstanding hypertension and chronic disease management.

Subjective
Michael Brooks is a 53-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. Hypertension has been present for approximately 16 years and is not currently under active management. Reports intermittent dizziness and palpitations. Denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, dyspnea, fatigue, or epistaxis. Nonsmoker. Relevant comorbidities include type 2 diabetes mellitus for 14 years and coronary artery disease for 3 years. Current medication regimen was not provided.

Objective
Height 166 cm; weight 59.5 kg; BMI 21.6 kg/m².
BP 147/89 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98%.
Serum sodium 138 mmol/L; potassium 4.2 mmol/L.

Assessment
Essential hypertension with above-target office blood pressure in the setting of longstanding untreated disease, type 2 diabetes, and coronary artery disease. Intermittent dizziness and palpitations without reported chest pain, dyspnea, or other acute hypertensive symptoms. Electrolytes are within expected limits.

Plan
Recommend re-establishing active blood pressure management, with home blood pressure monitoring and review of a documented log to confirm persistent elevation. Discuss initiation of antihypertensive therapy appropriate for coexisting diabetes and coronary artery disease after medication reconciliation and renal assessment.

Recommend ECG for evaluation of palpitations and cardiovascular risk assessment. Review lipid profile, urinalysis, renal function, and diabetes monitoring as part of chronic disease surveillance. Continue cardiovascular risk reduction with a low-sodium heart-healthy diet, regular physical activity as tolerated, moderation of alcohol if applicable, and maintenance of healthy weight.

Review preventive care, including age-appropriate cancer screening, immunizations, diabetic eye and foot surveillance, and routine cardiovascular follow-up. Seek urgent care for severe or persistent headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, focal neurologic symptoms, or markedly elevated blood pressure. Follow up after home readings and recommended investigations.
