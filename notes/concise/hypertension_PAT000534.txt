Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Subjective
Madelyn Stewart is a 54-year-old woman with a 9-year history of hypertension, currently without active management. She reports dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue. Denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, or epistaxis. Comorbid type 2 diabetes mellitus for 18 years and chronic kidney disease for 4 years. She currently smokes. No medication regimen was provided for review.

Objective
BP 157/97 mmHg, pulse 71 bpm, respirations 15/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 97%. Height 180 cm, weight 83.3 kg, BMI 25.7 kg/m². Serum sodium 145 mmol/L; potassium 4.1 mmol/L.

Assessment
Essential hypertension with persistent elevation and associated dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue. Longstanding hypertension is not currently controlled or actively managed. Diabetes and chronic kidney disease increase cardiovascular and renal risk. Current electrolytes are within expected limits.

Plan
Discussed need to re-establish active blood pressure treatment and close follow-up. Recommend home blood pressure monitoring with a written log. Review renal function and urine studies before and during therapy; consider an agent appropriate for concurrent diabetes and chronic kidney disease. Recommend ECG, lipid profile, and urinalysis as part of cardiovascular and hypertension evaluation. Counsel on sodium reduction, regular physical activity, weight maintenance, limiting alcohol if applicable, and smoking cessation. Reviewed emergency precautions for severe headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, neurologic symptoms, or markedly elevated blood pressure. Follow up promptly for treatment initiation and reassessment.
