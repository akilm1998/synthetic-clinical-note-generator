Chief Complaint

Annual review and follow-up of elevated blood pressure.

Subjective

Brian Simmons is a 52-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. He has a 9-year history of essential hypertension that has not been actively managed. He reports intermittent dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue. He denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, or nosebleeds. He has known coronary artery disease of approximately 4 years’ duration. He currently smokes. Weight is 77.9 kg at 168 cm (BMI 27.6 kg/m²).

Objective

Blood pressure 152/94 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respiratory rate 18/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 99%. Serum sodium 144 mmol/L and potassium 4.6 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension, persistently elevated and currently untreated, with associated intermittent dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue. No current symptoms suggesting hypertensive emergency. Cardiovascular risk is increased by known coronary artery disease, active smoking, and overweight status.

Coronary artery disease, chronic, reported duration 4 years.

Tobacco use.

Plan

Discussed the importance of active blood pressure management and cardiovascular risk reduction. Recommend home blood pressure monitoring with a written log and prompt follow-up to confirm ongoing elevation and establish treatment; medication therapy should be initiated or adjusted based on repeat readings and cardiovascular history. Obtain ECG, lipid profile, urinalysis, and renal function assessment as part of hypertension and coronary risk evaluation.

Counseled on reducing dietary sodium, regular physical activity as tolerated, weight reduction, limiting alcohol if applicable, and smoking cessation; cessation support offered. Reviewed warning symptoms requiring urgent evaluation, including chest pain, new shortness of breath, neurologic symptoms, severe headache, or markedly elevated blood pressure.

Continue comprehensive preventive care, including age-appropriate cancer screening and immunizations. Follow up soon for blood pressure review and cardiovascular risk management.
