Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for anemia.

Subjective
Shannon Douglas is a 32-year-old woman presenting for follow-up of 4-year anemia. She reports headache and denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, dyspnea, palpitations, reduced exercise tolerance, or other reported symptoms. She continues to smoke. Relevant comorbidities include rheumatoid arthritis, chronic kidney disease, and malignancy, each present for approximately 1 year. No current anemia-directed therapy is documented.

Objective
Height 175 cm, weight 67.4 kg, BMI 22.0 kg/m². BP 133/90 mmHg, pulse 87 bpm, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 97% on room air. Pallor noted.

Laboratory results: hemoglobin 8.6 g/dL, hematocrit 26.5%, RBC 3.05 million/µL, MCV 87 fL, serum iron 41 µg/dL, ferritin 359 ng/mL, TIBC 207 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 19.8%.

Assessment
Anemia of chronic inflammation, persistent and clinically significant, with normocytic indices. Low serum iron and TIBC with elevated ferritin support inflammation-related iron sequestration, likely contributed to by rheumatoid arthritis, chronic kidney disease, and malignancy. Headache may be related to anemia. No cardiopulmonary symptoms or hypoxemia reported.

Plan
Obtain CBC with reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia and assess contributing factors. Review and address control of rheumatoid arthritis, chronic kidney disease, and malignancy in coordination with the appropriate treating clinicians. Consider iron replacement strategy and erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy only after review of renal function, inflammatory status, malignancy context, and specialist recommendations. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Advise prompt evaluation for worsening headache, dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, or syncope. Close outpatient follow-up after evaluation.
