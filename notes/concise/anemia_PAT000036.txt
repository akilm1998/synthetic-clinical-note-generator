Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up of chronic anemia and review of laboratory results.

Subjective

Derek Black is an 82-year-old man with a 38-year history of anemia, type 2 diabetes mellitus for 44 years, and rheumatoid arthritis for 3 years. He reports no fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, pallor, reduced exercise tolerance, dyspnea, palpitations, or other cardiopulmonary symptoms. He does not smoke.

Objective

Height 171 cm, weight 85.4 kg, BMI 29.2 kg/m². Blood pressure 126/79 mmHg, pulse 78 bpm, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%.

Hemoglobin 9.3 g/dL, hematocrit 27.1%, RBC 3.15 million/µL, MCV 86 fL. Serum iron 42 µg/dL, ferritin 235 ng/mL, TIBC 203 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 20.7%. Cardiopulmonary status is clinically stable by vital signs.

Assessment

Chronic normocytic anemia, currently asymptomatic. The low serum iron with elevated ferritin and reduced TIBC is most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation, in the setting of rheumatoid arthritis. No evidence of symptomatic hemodynamic compromise today. His longstanding anemia is not currently under active management.

Plan

Obtain CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, along with vitamin B12, folate, renal function testing, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia and assess contributing factors. Review for occult blood loss and other causes of chronic anemia at follow-up. Optimize management of the underlying rheumatoid arthritis and diabetes in coordination with the treating clinicians. Consider hematology evaluation if anemia worsens, becomes symptomatic, or remains unexplained. Return promptly for dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, weakness, or bleeding.
