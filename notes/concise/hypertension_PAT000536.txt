Chief Complaint

Annual review and follow-up of longstanding hypertension.

Subjective

Jason Coleman is a 91-year-old man, 179 cm, 74.3 kg (BMI 23.2 kg/m²), presenting for comprehensive annual review. He has a 57-year history of essential hypertension that has not been actively managed. Reports intermittent dizziness and palpitations. Denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, fatigue, or epistaxis. Continues to smoke. Also has type 2 diabetes mellitus of 51 years’ duration.

Objective

Blood pressure 159/93 mmHg; pulse 74 bpm; respiratory rate 15/min; temperature 36.8°C; oxygen saturation 96%. Serum sodium 146 mmol/L and potassium 4.6 mmol/L. No additional examination findings documented.

Assessment

Essential hypertension with persistently elevated office blood pressure and associated intermittent dizziness and palpitations; currently untreated/not actively managed.

Type 2 diabetes mellitus, longstanding; current glycemic status not assessed in available data.

Current tobacco use.

Plan

Discussed cardiovascular and cerebrovascular risks of longstanding untreated hypertension, particularly in the setting of diabetes and smoking. Repeat seated blood pressure measurements and home blood pressure monitoring recommended. Arrange prompt clinician review to discuss initiation of antihypertensive therapy, with consideration of renal function and urine testing before and during treatment.

Recommend ECG evaluation for palpitations and dizziness, along with lipid profile and urinalysis as part of cardiovascular risk assessment. Counsel on smoking cessation, reduced sodium intake, regular activity as tolerated, and avoidance of excess alcohol.

Continue comprehensive diabetes follow-up, including glycemic assessment and routine eye and foot care. Review age-appropriate immunizations, fall risk, and advance care planning at the preventive care visit.

Seek urgent medical attention for severe or persistent headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, new neurologic symptoms, syncope, or markedly elevated blood pressure.
