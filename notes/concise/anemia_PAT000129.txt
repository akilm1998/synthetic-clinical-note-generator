Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective
Morgan Oliver is a 53-year-old woman, 166 cm, 76.3 kg (BMI 27.7 kg/m²), presenting for follow-up of longstanding anemia reported for approximately 22 years. She denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, reduced exercise tolerance, dyspnea, palpitations, or awareness of pallor. Comorbid chronic kidney disease for 5 years and inflammatory bowel disease for 6 years. She currently smokes. No active anemia-directed treatment is documented.

Objective
Vital signs: BP 125/83 mmHg, pulse 89 bpm, RR 22/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%.

Laboratory results: Hemoglobin 8.8 g/dL, hematocrit 25.7%, RBC 3.1 million/µL, MCV 83 fL, serum iron 49 µg/dL, ferritin 442 ng/mL, TIBC 260 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 18.8%.

Assessment
Chronic normocytic anemia, consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/disease in the setting of chronic kidney disease and inflammatory bowel disease. Hemoglobin is significantly reduced, with low serum iron and TIBC and elevated ferritin, supporting iron-restricted erythropoiesis associated with chronic disease rather than isolated absolute iron deficiency. Patient is currently asymptomatic and hemodynamically stable. Anemia has not been actively managed.

Plan
Obtain or review CBC with reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, vitamin B12, folate, renal function, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia. Coordinate management with nephrology and gastroenterology as clinically appropriate, including assessment of CKD and inflammatory bowel disease activity. Consider iron replacement strategy and erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy only after evaluation of contributing factors and specialist input. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Advise prompt evaluation for new dyspnea, chest pain, syncope, palpitations, bleeding, or worsening exercise intolerance. Reassess after diagnostic evaluation and initiation of an appropriate management plan.
