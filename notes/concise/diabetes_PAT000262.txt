Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for diabetes and elevated glucose readings.

Subjective
John Jacob is a 97-year-old man with a 51-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, currently inadequately controlled. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. He is a current smoker. History is also notable for depression of 55 years.

Objective
Height 171 cm, weight 85.7 kg, BMI 29.3 kg/m². Blood pressure 124/78 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respiratory rate 15/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 95%. HbA1c 9.2%; fasting glucose 219 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications, with persistent hyperglycemia after a prolonged disease course. He is presently asymptomatic, with no reported neuropathic, infectious, visual, or constitutional complications. Overweight status and ongoing tobacco use increase overall cardiometabolic risk. Chronic depression is also noted.

Plan
Review and reconcile the current diabetes regimen and promptly initiate or intensify therapy as clinically appropriate, with particular attention to avoiding hypoglycemia given advanced age. Encourage individualized nutrition counseling, safe physical activity, and home glucose monitoring. Recommend assessment of renal function and urine albumin, fasting lipids, and routine diabetic eye and foot care if not recently completed. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Continue monitoring mood and functional status related to longstanding depression. Close outpatient follow-up is recommended to reassess glucose control and treatment tolerance.
