Chief Complaint
Annual review of diabetes and chronic conditions.

Subjective
Jason Coleman is a 97-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. Type 2 diabetes mellitus for approximately 22 years, currently poorly controlled with limited active management. He denies increased thirst, polyuria, increased hunger, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, or foot numbness. Reports tingling in the feet. Hypertension has been present for 8 years. History of depression for 72 months. Nonsmoker.

Objective
Height 173 cm; weight 63.7 kg; BMI 21.3 kg/m².
BP 141/85 mmHg, pulse 68 bpm, respirations 19/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%.
HbA1c 9.6%; fasting glucose 231 mg/dL.

Assessment
1. Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, suboptimally controlled. HbA1c and fasting glucose are significantly elevated despite absence of prominent hyperglycemic symptoms. Chronic intermittent distal paresthesia noted.
2. Hypertension, mildly above goal at today’s visit.
3. Depression, longstanding.
4. Preventive health and functional risk review appropriate given advanced age.

Plan
Discussed diabetes results and risks of sustained hyperglycemia. Review current medications, adherence, diet, glucose-monitoring practices, and goals with patient/caregiver; consider initiation or adjustment of glucose-lowering therapy with an individualized, safety-focused target given age and frailty risk. Avoid hypoglycemia and excessive treatment burden.

Recommend renal assessment with urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, and routine diabetic eye and foot examinations. Counsel on daily foot inspection, appropriate footwear, nutrition, hydration, and recognition of hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia. Evaluate persistent tingling at follow-up.

Reassess blood pressure with home or repeat office measurements and review antihypertensive regimen if applicable. Screen depression symptoms, treatment response, cognition, fall risk, functional status, and social supports. Review immunizations, cancer screening appropriateness, vision/hearing, advance care planning, and goals of care. Follow up after medication and monitoring review, sooner for symptomatic hyperglycemia, hypoglycemia, worsening paresthesias, or foot wounds.
