Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Julian Holland is a 72-year-old man with a 28-year history of type 2 diabetes, obesity, hypertension, and nonalcoholic fatty liver disease. Diabetes has not been actively managed recently. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the hands or feet. He does not smoke.

Objective

Height 178 cm, weight 97.6 kg, BMI 30.8 kg/m². Blood pressure 144/83 mmHg, pulse 77 bpm, respiratory rate 22/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98%. HbA1c 9.2%; fasting glucose 193 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications, currently poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. No hyperglycemic, neuropathic, infectious, or constitutional symptoms reported.

Obesity, BMI 30.8 kg/m², longstanding.

Hypertension with mildly elevated office blood pressure.

Longstanding nonalcoholic fatty liver disease.

Plan

Discussed elevated glucose levels and the importance of re-establishing consistent diabetes management. Review and initiate an appropriate glucose-lowering regimen at follow-up, with attention to renal function and comorbidities. Counsel on reducing concentrated carbohydrates, portion control, weight loss, and regular physical activity as tolerated. Recommend home glucose monitoring and review of readings.

Recommend screening for diabetic kidney disease, fasting lipids, renal function, and routine diabetic eye and foot evaluations.

Reinforce blood pressure monitoring, low-sodium diet, weight reduction, and medication review for hypertension.

Continue lifestyle-focused management of obesity and fatty liver disease. Follow up after treatment planning and updated monitoring.
