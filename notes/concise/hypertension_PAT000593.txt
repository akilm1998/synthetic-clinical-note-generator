Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Subjective

Avery Ward is an 88-year-old woman with approximately 40 years of hypertension and chronic kidney disease for 13 years. She is not currently receiving treatment for blood pressure. She denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, or nosebleeds. She does not smoke. Weight is stable at 51.8 kg; BMI 21.0 kg/m².

Objective

Blood pressure 165/97 mmHg. Pulse 69 bpm. Respiratory rate 18/min. Temperature 36.6°C. Oxygen saturation 97% on room air. Height 157 cm; weight 51.8 kg. Serum sodium 138 mmol/L and potassium 3.8 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension, persistently elevated and currently untreated. No symptoms to suggest hypertensive emergency today. Longstanding hypertension with concomitant chronic kidney disease. Electrolytes are within normal limits.

Plan

Repeat blood pressure after rest and obtain home readings if feasible. Discuss initiation of antihypertensive therapy, with cautious titration appropriate for advanced age and chronic kidney disease. Review renal function before and during treatment; consider urinalysis and ECG as part of cardiovascular and renal risk assessment. Counsel on limiting dietary sodium, avoiding excess alcohol, and maintaining regular activity as tolerated. Review precautions for severe headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, neurologic symptoms, or markedly worsening blood pressure, which would require urgent evaluation. Follow up soon for blood pressure reassessment and treatment planning.
