Chief Complaint
Initial consultation for anemia.

Subjective
Noah Hamilton is an 84-year-old man presenting for evaluation of anemia reportedly present for approximately 36 years. He reports headache and appears to have pallor. He denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, shortness of breath, palpitations, and reduced exercise tolerance. History is notable for heart failure for 23 years and type 2 diabetes mellitus for 37 years. He currently smokes. No medication history is provided.

Objective
Height 168 cm, weight 68.9 kg, BMI 24.4 kg/m². BP 133/76 mmHg, pulse 91 bpm, respiratory rate 22/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

General: Pallor noted.
Cardiovascular: Normal examination.
Respiratory: Normal examination.

Laboratory results:
Hemoglobin 8.4 g/dL; hematocrit 25.8%; RBC 2.77 million/µL; MCV 93 fL. Serum iron 37 µg/dL, ferritin 477 ng/mL, TIBC 253 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 14.6%.

Assessment
Anemia consistent with chronic disease/inflammation: longstanding, normocytic anemia with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and reduced TIBC. Chronic heart failure and diabetes may contribute to ongoing inflammatory or renal-associated anemia. Headache and pallor are present, without current cardiopulmonary symptoms or hypoxemia.

Plan
Obtain/review CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, renal function, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers to further characterize the anemia and assess reversible contributors. Review for chronic inflammatory, renal, infectious, or occult blood-loss sources as clinically indicated. Consider hematology consultation given hemoglobin of 8.4 g/dL and prolonged duration. Discuss smoking cessation and monitor for new dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, syncope, or worsening exercise tolerance; seek urgent care if these occur. Reassess after initial evaluation to establish an appropriate management plan.
