Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective
Brooke Snyder is a 62-year-old woman with a 19-year history of anemia in the setting of longstanding rheumatoid arthritis and malignancy. She reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, shortness of breath, palpitations, or reduced exercise tolerance. Pallor is noted. Nonsmoker. No current anemia-directed treatment is documented.

Objective
Vital signs: BP 134/80 mmHg, pulse 77 bpm, respirations 21/min, temperature 36.9°C, SpO2 99% on room air. Height 155 cm, weight 68.7 kg, BMI 28.6 kg/m². Pallor present.

Laboratory results: Hemoglobin 8.2 g/dL, hematocrit 24.0%, RBC 2.79 million/µL, MCV 86 fL. Serum iron 42 µg/dL, ferritin 353 ng/mL, TIBC 200 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 21%.

Assessment
Chronic normocytic anemia, most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation associated with rheumatoid arthritis and malignancy. Hemoglobin remains significantly reduced, although she is currently without cardiopulmonary or constitutional symptoms. Condition has not been actively managed.

Plan
Review anemia and laboratory findings with the patient. Recommend evaluation with CBC including reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, renal function, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers, with continued monitoring of hemoglobin and hematocrit. Recommend coordination with hematology/oncology and rheumatology regarding underlying malignancy and rheumatoid arthritis and consideration of anemia-directed therapy, including iron or erythropoiesis-stimulating treatment if clinically appropriate. Advise urgent evaluation for new or worsening shortness of breath, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, syncope, or marked weakness.
