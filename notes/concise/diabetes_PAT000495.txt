Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for diabetes and intermittent tingling in hands/feet.

Subjective
Amelia Foster is a 39-year-old woman with a 4-year history of type 2 diabetes, currently not receiving documented management. Recent HbA1c is 10.0% and fasting glucose 209 mg/dL. Reports tingling in the hands/feet. Denies increased thirst, polyuria, increased hunger, weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. History notable for obesity for approximately 20 years and anxiety for 17 years. Current smoker.

Objective
Height 165 cm; weight 94.5 kg; BMI 34.7 kg/m².
BP 124/76 mmHg, pulse 80/min, respirations 20/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 97%.
HbA1c 10.0%; fasting glucose 209 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complication, poorly controlled based on current glycemic values. Tingling in hands/feet raises concern for early peripheral sensory symptoms, though no formal neuropathy diagnosis established. Obesity. Tobacco use. Anxiety history.

Plan
Discussed significance of elevated glucose and HbA1c and need for prompt active diabetes management. Recommend initiation of appropriate glucose-lowering therapy, with medication selection after review of renal function, contraindications, preferences, and access. Begin home glucose monitoring and provide nutrition, weight-management, and physical-activity counseling.

Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine/eGFR, fasting lipid profile, comprehensive foot examination, and dilated retinal screening. Counsel on daily foot inspection and smoking cessation. Review hyperglycemia warning symptoms and advise urgent evaluation for vomiting, abdominal pain, confusion, marked weakness, or inability to maintain hydration. Close outpatient follow-up for treatment initiation and glucose review.
