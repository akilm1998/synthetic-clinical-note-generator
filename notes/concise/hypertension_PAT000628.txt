Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Subjective

Rebecca Burke is an 87-year-old woman with approximately 40 years of hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus for 36 years. She is not currently receiving treatment for hypertension. She reports intermittent dizziness and fatigue. She denies headache, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, or nosebleeds. She continues to smoke. No medication regimen was provided for review.

Objective

Height 164 cm, weight 71.3 kg, BMI 26.5 kg/m². Blood pressure 163/90 mmHg, pulse 79 bpm, respirations 20/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98%. Serum sodium 141 mmol/L and potassium 3.8 mmol/L.

Assessment

Longstanding essential hypertension with elevated blood pressure and suboptimal control in the setting of no current treatment. Dizziness and fatigue are present without symptoms suggesting acute hypertensive emergency. Type 2 diabetes mellitus and active tobacco use increase cardiovascular risk.

Plan

Discussed the importance of treating her persistently elevated blood pressure and reviewed risks of untreated hypertension. Recommend home blood pressure monitoring with attention to dizziness or orthostatic symptoms. Arrange prompt outpatient follow-up to review readings and initiate an age-appropriate antihypertensive regimen, with cautious titration. Recommend baseline renal function assessment, urinalysis, lipid profile, and ECG as clinically appropriate before or during treatment initiation. Counsel regarding smoking cessation, reduced sodium intake, physical activity as tolerated, and weight maintenance. Advised urgent evaluation for chest pain, shortness of breath, severe headache, new neurologic symptoms, or significant vision changes.
