Chief Complaint
Annual review of anemia.

Subjective
Logan Stewart is a 48-year-old male, nonsmoker, presenting for annual review of anemia present for approximately 2 years. He denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, reduced exercise tolerance, dyspnea, palpitations, or symptoms suggestive of pallor. History is notable for malignancy of 8 years’ duration. No current anemia-directed therapy is documented.

Objective
Height 169 cm, weight 68.3 kg, BMI 23.9 kg/m².
BP 119/81 mmHg, pulse 85 bpm, respiratory rate 19/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98%.
Hemoglobin 9.7 g/dL, hematocrit 29.3%, RBC 3.22 million/µL, MCV 91 fL. Serum iron 47 µg/dL, ferritin 197 ng/mL, TIBC 264 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 17.8%.

Assessment
Persistent normocytic anemia with low serum iron and transferrin saturation and relatively preserved ferritin, most consistent with anemia associated with chronic inflammation/malignancy. Currently clinically compensated and asymptomatic. No evidence of acute cardiopulmonary compromise based on vital signs and reported symptoms.

Plan
Obtain/review CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear, renal function, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers; repeat iron studies as clinically indicated.
Assess for interval change or active treatment related to the underlying malignancy and coordinate management with the treating oncology team.
No empiric iron regimen documented today; consider oral or intravenous iron only after evaluation of iron availability and the underlying inflammatory process.
Advise prompt review for new dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, bleeding, or worsening functional tolerance.
Continue routine annual preventive care, including age-appropriate cancer screening and immunization review. Follow up after laboratory reassessment or sooner if symptoms develop.
