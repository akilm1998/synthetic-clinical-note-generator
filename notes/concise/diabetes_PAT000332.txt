Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective
Jasmine Spencer is a 44-year-old woman presenting for routine diabetes follow-up. Type 2 diabetes has been present for approximately 2 years and is currently untreated. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the extremities. She does not smoke. History is also notable for obstructive sleep apnea for 3 years.

Objective
Height 169 cm, weight 72.0 kg, BMI 25.2 kg/m². Blood pressure 118/77 mmHg, pulse 72 bpm, respiratory rate 16/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 97%. HbA1c 9.3%; fasting glucose 208 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. No reported hyperglycemic, neuropathic, or infectious symptoms. Obstructive sleep apnea, chronic.

Plan
Discussed laboratory results and need for active diabetes management. Recommend initiating pharmacologic therapy, with metformin considered if no contraindication, along with a structured nutrition plan, portion control, and regular physical activity. Encourage home glucose monitoring and review of glucose goals at follow-up. Recommend assessment of renal function and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, fasting lipid profile, comprehensive foot examination, and routine diabetic retinal screening. Continue management of obstructive sleep apnea. Follow up in approximately 3 months to reassess glycemic control, sooner for worsening symptoms.
