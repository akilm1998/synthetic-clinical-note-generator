Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective
Melanie Knight is a 76-year-old woman with a 29-year history of type 2 diabetes, hypertension for 26 years, depression for 34 years, and obesity for 6 years. She reports no increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling. She continues to smoke. No current diabetes medication regimen was provided.

Objective
Height 161 cm; weight 101.1 kg; BMI 39.0 kg/m². Blood pressure 147/85 mmHg, pulse 80 bpm, respiratory rate 24/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%. HbA1c 9.7%; fasting glucose 256 mg/dL.

Assessment
Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, currently poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Patient is presently asymptomatic for hyperglycemia. Severe obesity and longstanding hypertension increase cardiovascular and metabolic risk. Tobacco use.

Plan
Discussed need for active diabetes management and reduction of dietary carbohydrate intake, portion control, and regular low-impact physical activity as tolerated. Review and initiate an appropriate glucose-lowering regimen, including consideration of metformin or other agents, after medication reconciliation and assessment of renal function. Recommend home glucose monitoring and review of readings at follow-up. Obtain renal function, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, and fasting lipid profile as appropriate for diabetes risk assessment. Encourage routine diabetic eye and foot care. Address smoking cessation. Reassess blood pressure and diabetes control at close outpatient follow-up.
