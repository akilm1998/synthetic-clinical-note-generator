Chief Complaint
Annual review of hypertension and chronic medical conditions.

Subjective
Tristan Burke is a 94-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. Essential hypertension has been present for approximately 31 years and is not currently under active management. He reports fatigue but denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, or epistaxis. Current smoking reported. History also includes hyperlipidemia for 62 years and coronary artery disease for 3 years. No antihypertensive regimen is documented.

Objective
Height 173 cm, weight 65.5 kg, BMI 21.9 kg/m². Blood pressure 159/94 mmHg, pulse 81 bpm, respiratory rate 16/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%. Serum sodium 136 mmol/L and potassium 4.2 mmol/L.

Assessment
Essential hypertension with persistently elevated office blood pressure and no active treatment, currently without symptoms suggestive of acute hypertensive complication.

Coronary artery disease, chronic, requiring consideration when selecting and titrating antihypertensive therapy.

Hyperlipidemia.

Chronic fatigue, nonspecific.

Active tobacco use.

Plan
Confirm blood pressure with repeat office measurement and home readings if feasible. Review prior treatment history and discuss initiation of antihypertensive therapy, with cautious titration given advanced age and coronary artery disease. Monitor electrolytes and renal function after treatment initiation or adjustment.

Recommend ECG, urinalysis, and lipid profile as part of cardiovascular risk assessment; review results when available. Reinforce smoking cessation, avoidance of excess dietary sodium, regular activity as tolerated, and fall-prevention measures.

Continue comprehensive preventive care, including individualized immunization and cancer-screening review based on overall health and preferences. Follow up promptly for chest pain, dyspnea, neurologic symptoms, severe headache, or markedly elevated blood pressure; otherwise return for blood pressure reassessment and chronic disease follow-up.
