Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for chronic kidney disease.

Subjective
Sophie Bryant is a 45-year-old woman with an approximately 8-year history of chronic kidney disease, presenting for routine follow-up. She reports persistent fatigue. She denies pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, peripheral edema, changes in urinary frequency, sleep disturbance, or shortness of breath. Chronic kidney disease has not been under active management. Relevant comorbidities include anemia of chronic disease for 5 years and secondary hyperparathyroidism for 1 year. She reports current smoking. Weight is 69.2 kg; BMI 26.7 kg/m².

Objective
Vital signs: BP 143/87 mmHg, pulse 81 bpm, respiratory rate 16/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Height 161 cm, weight 69.2 kg.

Recent laboratory results: serum creatinine 2.55 mg/dL, eGFR 19 mL/min/1.73 m², BUN 87 mg/dL.

Assessment
Chronic kidney disease with substantially reduced renal function and elevated BUN/creatinine, currently without reported volume-overload or prominent uremic symptoms aside from fatigue. Disease control and renal follow-up appear inadequate. Associated anemia of chronic disease and secondary hyperparathyroidism. Blood pressure is above goal. Active tobacco use.

Plan
Recommend prompt nephrology evaluation for comprehensive CKD management and renal replacement therapy education as clinically appropriate. Obtain repeat renal function and serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound. Review blood pressure management and avoid NSAIDs and other nephrotoxins. Counsel on smoking cessation, sodium moderation, and prompt evaluation for worsening fatigue, nausea, pruritus, edema, reduced urine output, dyspnea, or confusion. Continue close outpatient follow-up after updated assessment.
