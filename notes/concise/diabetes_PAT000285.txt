Chief Complaint

Annual review of diabetes and chronic conditions.

Subjective

Frank Montgomery is a 34-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review. Type 2 diabetes has been present for approximately 14 years and is currently poorly controlled without an established management regimen documented. Recent HbA1c is 9.9% and fasting glucose 243 mg/dL. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling.

History also includes obesity for 10 years, anxiety for 3 years, and depression for 1 year. He currently smokes. No acute concerns reported.

Objective

Height 160 cm; weight 78.1 kg; BMI 30.5 kg/m².
BP 120/80 mmHg, pulse 84 bpm, respirations 17/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 98%.
HbA1c 9.9%; fasting glucose 243 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, inadequately controlled based on markedly elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Despite absence of hyperglycemic or neuropathic symptoms, prolonged disease duration and current glycemic burden increase risk for renal, retinal, neurologic, and cardiovascular complications.

Obesity, BMI 30.5 kg/m².

Tobacco use.

History of anxiety and depression.

Plan

Discussed importance of active diabetes management, nutrition changes, weight reduction, and regular physical activity. Recommend prompt initiation or adjustment of guideline-directed glucose-lowering therapy after review of renal function, current medication history, and treatment preferences.

Recommend monitoring fasting and postprandial glucose as clinically appropriate and repeat HbA1c after treatment changes. Obtain renal assessment including serum creatinine/eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, fasting lipid profile, and routine diabetes monitoring.

Recommend annual dilated retinal examination and comprehensive diabetic foot examination. Review immunizations and preventive health measures at the annual visit.

Counseled regarding smoking cessation and reduction of cardiovascular risk. Address anxiety and depression with routine symptom and safety assessment and offer appropriate behavioral health support. Follow up soon for diabetes treatment planning and reassessment.
