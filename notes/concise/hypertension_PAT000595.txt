Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Subjective
Evan Bryant is a 42-year-old man with a 6-year history of hypertension, currently without active management. He denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, or epistaxis. Current comorbidities include type 2 diabetes mellitus for 2 years, coronary artery disease for 1 year, and chronic kidney disease for 1 year. He reports ongoing tobacco use.

Objective
Height 171 cm, weight 77.2 kg, BMI 26.4 kg/m². Blood pressure 167/94 mmHg, pulse 83 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 97%. Serum sodium 139 mmol/L and potassium 4.5 mmol/L.

Assessment
Essential hypertension with markedly elevated office blood pressure and no reported symptoms of acute end-organ involvement. Hypertension is not currently being actively managed. Increased cardiovascular and renal risk given coexisting diabetes, coronary artery disease, chronic kidney disease, and ongoing tobacco use.

Plan
Confirm blood pressure with repeat measurement and home monitoring. Discuss prompt initiation of an appropriate antihypertensive regimen, with medication selection guided by diabetes, coronary artery disease, and chronic kidney disease. Review adherence and potential barriers at follow-up.

Counseled on reduced-sodium diet, regular physical activity as tolerated, weight control, moderation of alcohol if applicable, and smoking cessation. Recommend review of renal function, urinalysis, lipid profile, and ECG as part of cardiovascular risk assessment and hypertension management. Continue follow-up in the near term for blood pressure reassessment; seek urgent care for chest pain, shortness of breath, neurologic symptoms, severe headache, or markedly worsening blood pressure.
