Assessment
1. Hyperlipidemia with markedly elevated LDL-C and triglycerides and low HDL-C. Persistent severe mixed dyslipidemia with reported xanthomas, in the setting of diabetes and established coronary artery disease. No current ischemic or claudication symptoms.
2. Coronary artery disease, established comorbidity; currently without reported chest discomfort.
3. Type 2 diabetes mellitus and hypertension, important cardiovascular risk comorbidities.

Plan
Discussed the significantly elevated cardiovascular risk and need for active lipid-lowering therapy. Recommend initiation of a high-intensity statin after medication reconciliation and review of contraindications; consider adding ezetimibe if LDL remains above goal. Repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation or adjustment and assess for secondary contributors to dyslipidemia. Counsel on a heart-healthy diet low in saturated and trans fats, limiting refined carbohydrates, regular physical activity, and weight maintenance. Continue monitoring for chest pain, exertional leg symptoms, or other cardiovascular complaints. Close outpatient follow-up for lipid and cardiovascular risk management.
