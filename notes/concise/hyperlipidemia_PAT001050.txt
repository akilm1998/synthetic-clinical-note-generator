Hypertension with blood pressure above goal today.

Chronic kidney disease, longstanding.

Tobacco use.

Plan

Discussed substantially elevated cholesterol and associated cardiovascular risk. Recommend initiation of high-intensity statin therapy after shared decision-making, with consideration of additional lipid-lowering therapy if response is inadequate or treatment is not tolerated. Repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation to assess response. Consider evaluation for secondary contributors and additional cardiovascular risk assessment as clinically appropriate.

Continue outpatient monitoring and management of hypertension and chronic kidney disease, with attention to renal function when selecting and monitoring lipid-lowering therapy. Follow up after treatment initiation to review tolerance, blood pressure, and lipid response.
