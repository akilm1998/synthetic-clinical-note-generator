Chief Complaint

Medication review and follow-up of chronic kidney disease.

Subjective

Nicole Harper is a 59-year-old woman with approximately 16 years of chronic kidney disease and 20-year history of hypertension, here for medication effectiveness, adherence, and tolerance review. Current medication regimen is not provided for review. She reports fatigue. Denies pruritus, muscle cramps, nausea, sleep disturbance, peripheral edema, change in urination, or shortness of breath. Current smoker. No medication adverse effects reported.

Objective

Height 173 cm, weight 69.4 kg, BMI 23.2 kg/m². BP 148/96 mmHg, pulse 76/min, respirations 15/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%. Serum creatinine 5.61 mg/dL, eGFR 7 mL/min/1.73 m², BUN 75 mg/dL.

Assessment

Chronic kidney disease with markedly reduced renal function and associated fatigue. No current symptoms of fluid overload or prominent uremic symptoms reported. Blood pressure remains above goal. Medication effectiveness and adherence cannot be confirmed without a complete medication reconciliation.

Plan

Complete medication reconciliation, including adherence, dosing, renal appropriateness, and adverse effects. Avoid NSAIDs and other nephrotoxic medications; review all over-the-counter and herbal products. Recommend prompt nephrology evaluation given current renal function and discussion of renal replacement therapy planning. Recommend monitoring with serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound as clinically indicated. Address blood pressure management after medication review. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Seek urgent care for new shortness of breath, swelling, persistent vomiting, confusion, chest pain, or markedly decreased urination.
