Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for longstanding anemia.

Subjective
Joel Pierce is a 95-year-old male, nonsmoker, seen for follow-up of anemia present for approximately 35 years. He reports intermittent headache but denies fatigue, weakness, dizziness, dyspnea, palpitations, reduced exercise tolerance, or other reported symptoms. Anemia has not been actively managed. Relevant chronic conditions include inflammatory bowel disease for 32 years, rheumatoid arthritis for 21 years, and chronic kidney disease for 8 years. No medication regimen is provided.

Objective
Height 166 cm; weight 59.5 kg; BMI 21.6 kg/m².
BP 138/84 mmHg, pulse 78 bpm, RR 21/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 97%.
Hemoglobin 9.2 g/dL, hematocrit 28.3%, RBC 3.11 million/µL, MCV 91 fL. Serum iron 49 µg/dL, ferritin 305 ng/mL, TIBC 209 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 23.4%.

Assessment
Longstanding normocytic anemia consistent with anemia of chronic disease/inflammation, likely related to chronic inflammatory bowel disease, rheumatoid arthritis, and chronic kidney disease. Hemoglobin remains reduced, though patient reports minimal associated symptoms. Headache noted without accompanying dizziness or cardiopulmonary complaints.

Plan
Discussed laboratory pattern and likely contribution from chronic inflammation and kidney disease. Recommend initiation of structured outpatient evaluation and management with repeat CBC, reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, renal function testing, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers. Review for occult blood loss and nutritional factors given inflammatory bowel disease. Consider renal and/or hematology consultation if anemia persists or worsens, and assess candidacy for iron therapy or erythropoiesis-stimulating treatment based on follow-up evaluation. Advise prompt evaluation for worsening headache, dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, bleeding, or marked weakness. Follow up after laboratory reassessment.
