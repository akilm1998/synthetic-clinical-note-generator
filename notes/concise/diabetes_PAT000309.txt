Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for diabetes.

Subjective

Stella Gray is a 55-year-old woman seen for follow-up of type 2 diabetes diagnosed approximately 24 years ago. Diabetes control remains suboptimal. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, blurred vision, fatigue, unintentional weight loss, slow-healing wounds, or recurrent infections. Reports numbness and tingling in the hands and feet. No current diabetes treatment regimen is documented. She continues to smoke.

Relevant comorbidities include obesity, anxiety, and chronic kidney disease.

Objective

Height 152 cm; weight 81.1 kg; BMI 35.1 kg/m². Blood pressure 138/80 mmHg, pulse 85 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 96%.

HbA1c 9.3%. Fasting glucose 210 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications, poorly controlled based on elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose. Paresthesias of the hands and feet are present.

Obesity.

Chronic kidney disease, longstanding.

Tobacco use.

Anxiety.

Plan

Discussed elevated glucose values and importance of active diabetes management. Review and initiate an appropriate glucose-lowering regimen, with medication selection and dosing guided by renal function. Encourage home glucose monitoring, reduced refined carbohydrate intake, portion control, and gradual increase in physical activity as tolerated.

Recommend renal monitoring with serum creatinine/eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, along with fasting lipid profile. Recommend routine diabetic foot assessment and dilated retinal examination. Counsel regarding daily foot care and prompt evaluation of wounds or infection.

Encouraged smoking cessation and weight reduction. Continue follow-up for diabetes and chronic kidney disease, with reassessment after treatment changes.
