Plan

Arrange urgent nephrology evaluation for advanced chronic kidney disease, assessment of renal replacement therapy needs, and development of an appropriate management plan. Discussed that absence of symptoms does not exclude serious renal dysfunction.

Complete medication reconciliation at the next contact using all prescription bottles, over-the-counter products, supplements, and pharmacy records. Confirm indication, dose, adherence, benefit, and adverse effects for every medication. Renally adjust medications as appropriate and avoid NSAIDs and other potentially nephrotoxic agents unless specifically directed by a treating clinician. Do not start or stop prescription therapy without review.

Recommend serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound as part of comprehensive kidney disease evaluation. These are recommendations and have not been documented as completed today.

Continue monitoring blood pressure and review cardiovascular risk-reduction therapy in the context of coronary artery disease. Encourage smoking cessation.

Review gout treatment for renal dosing and avoid unsupervised NSAID use.

Seek emergency care immediately for new shortness of breath, chest pain, confusion, persistent vomiting, marked weakness, new swelling, substantially decreased urination, or other acute deterioration.

Close outpatient follow-up after medication reconciliation and nephrology assessment.
