Plan

Arrange prompt nephrology follow-up for management of advanced chronic kidney disease and counseling regarding renal replacement therapy planning if clinically appropriate. Recommend monitoring serum electrolytes, CBC, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, and intact parathyroid hormone, with renal ultrasound as clinically indicated. Continue management of anemia and secondary hyperparathyroidism in coordination with nephrology.

Counseled regarding smoking cessation and avoidance of nonprescribed medications or supplements that may worsen kidney function. Follow up promptly after medication reconciliation and renal evaluation.
