Plan

Obtain an updated medication and supplement reconciliation, with particular attention to antihypertensive therapy, renal dosing, over-the-counter medications, and NSAID use. Avoid NSAIDs and other potentially nephrotoxic medications unless specifically approved by a treating clinician.

Seek urgent care for new or worsening shortness of breath, chest pain, confusion, persistent vomiting, markedly decreased urination, or rapidly progressive swelling. Arrange close outpatient follow-up after repeat evaluation and nephrology consultation.
