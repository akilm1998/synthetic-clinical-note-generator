Assessment

Essential hypertension, longstanding and currently inadequately controlled based on today’s blood pressure of 154/97 mmHg. He is asymptomatic for acute hypertensive complications, without chest pain, dyspnea, neurologic symptoms, visual changes, or epistaxis. Electrolytes are within the reported reference range. Given his age and persistent elevation, treatment should be initiated thoughtfully with attention to orthostatic symptoms and renal function.

Hyperlipidemia, longstanding, with no current lipid values or treatment details provided.

Fatigue, nonspecific and without associated cardiopulmonary or neurologic symptoms reported today.

Current tobacco use, a modifiable cardiovascular risk factor.

Plan

Confirm blood pressure control with repeat measurements, including home blood pressure monitoring if feasible, using appropriate technique. Assess for orthostatic blood pressure changes given advanced age and the anticipated initiation of therapy.

Discuss initiation of antihypertensive medication, with selection and dosing individualized to age, comorbid risk, renal function, and tolerance. Review potential adverse effects and reinforce the importance of adherence. Obtain baseline renal function and additional metabolic testing as clinically appropriate before and after treatment initiation.

Recommend a low-sodium, heart-healthy diet, regular physical activity as tolerated, moderation of alcohol if applicable, and maintenance of current healthy weight. Counsel regarding smoking cessation and offer cessation support.

Recommend cardiovascular risk assessment with lipid profile, urinalysis, and ECG as appropriate for longstanding hypertension and hyperlipidemia. Review for evidence of target-organ involvement at subsequent visits.

Reassess fatigue after blood pressure evaluation and initial management; advise earlier evaluation for new chest pain, shortness of breath, focal neurologic symptoms, severe headache, or vision changes.

Follow up in the near term for blood pressure reassessment and treatment adjustment.
