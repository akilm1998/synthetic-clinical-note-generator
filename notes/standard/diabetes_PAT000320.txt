Chief Complaint

Annual review of diabetes and chronic medical conditions.

Subjective

Anna Cook is a 55-year-old woman presenting for comprehensive annual review. She has a 13-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus and is not currently receiving documented active management. Recent laboratory testing shows HbA1c 9.2% and fasting glucose 219 mg/dL, consistent with persistent hyperglycemia.

She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the hands or feet. No smoking history. Obstructive sleep apnea has been present for approximately 11 years; current treatment and symptom control should be reviewed.

Objective

Height 151 cm; weight 53.1 kg; BMI 23.3 kg/m².

Blood pressure 117/76 mmHg
Pulse 69 bpm
Respiratory rate 14/min
Temperature 36.7°C
Oxygen saturation 97%

Laboratory results:
HbA1c 9.2%
Fasting glucose 219 mg/dL

Assessment

1. Type 2 diabetes mellitus without documented complications

Diabetes is substantially above goal based on HbA1c of 9.2% and fasting glucose of 219 mg/dL. She is currently asymptomatic, with no reported neuropathic symptoms, visual symptoms, recurrent infections, or impaired wound healing. Despite a normal BMI of 23.3 kg/m², the duration of diabetes and degree of hyperglycemia warrant prompt treatment escalation and assessment for microvascular and cardiovascular risk factors.

2. Obstructive sleep apnea

Longstanding obstructive sleep apnea, approximately 11 years in duration. Current symptom burden and adherence to prescribed therapy require review.

3. Preventive health maintenance

Annual preventive care reviewed in the context of diabetes, including cardiovascular risk reduction, immunizations, cancer screening, eye care, and foot care.

Plan

Discussed that the current glucose values indicate inadequately controlled diabetes and carry increased long-term risk for renal, retinal, neurologic, and cardiovascular complications. Emphasized nutrition focused on reducing refined carbohydrates and added sugars, regular physical activity as tolerated, and continued avoidance of tobacco. Although weight is not elevated, dietary quality and activity remain important for glycemic control.

Arrange prompt medication review and initiation or escalation of glucose-lowering therapy. Metformin would be a reasonable first-line option if renal function and other contraindications permit; treatment selection should be individualized after medication reconciliation and review of renal function. Discuss home glucose monitoring and recognition of symptomatic hyperglycemia and hypoglycemia.

Obtain urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine with estimated glomerular filtration rate, and fasting lipid profile for diabetes risk assessment. Repeat HbA1c after approximately 3 months of active treatment.

Recommend comprehensive dilated retinal examination and a complete diabetic foot examination, including assessment of skin integrity, sensation, and peripheral pulses. Review daily foot care and advise prompt evaluation of any wound or infection.

Review cardiovascular risk factors at follow-up. Blood pressure is currently 117/76 mmHg. Confirm immunizations are up to date, including influenza, COVID-19, pneumococcal, recombinant zoster, and tetanus vaccination as appropriate. Ensure age-appropriate breast, colorectal, and cervical cancer screening is current.

Review current management of obstructive sleep apnea, including treatment adherence, residual daytime sleepiness, and sleep quality. Consider reassessment if symptoms are not adequately controlled.

Follow up after recommended laboratory evaluation and medication reconciliation, with diabetes-focused follow-up in approximately 3 months or sooner for worsening symptoms.
