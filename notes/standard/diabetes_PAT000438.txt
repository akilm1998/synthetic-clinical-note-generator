Assessment

Despite the absence of hyperglycemic, constitutional, infectious, or neuropathic symptoms, the HbA1c and fasting glucose indicate clinically significant persistent hyperglycemia. At age 88, treatment goals should be individualized with emphasis on symptomatic benefit, avoidance of hypoglycemia, regimen simplicity, and consideration of overall health and functional status.

Obstructive sleep apnea, longstanding, approximately 9 years.

Plan
Review current diabetes treatment, adherence, dietary pattern, home glucose monitoring, and barriers to care at the next medication reconciliation; no active diabetes regimen is documented in the supplied history.
