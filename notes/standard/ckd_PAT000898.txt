Chief Complaint
Routine follow-up for chronic kidney disease.

Subjective
Maxwell Knight is a 34-year-old man presenting for outpatient follow-up of chronic kidney disease, diagnosed approximately 14 years ago. He reports ongoing fatigue. He denies pruritus, nausea, muscle cramps, sleep disturbance, peripheral edema, change in urinary frequency, or shortness of breath. No tobacco use. Comorbidity includes gout for approximately 1 year. Current medication regimen is not provided.

Objective
Height 162 cm; weight 65.3 kg; BMI 24.9 kg/m².
Blood pressure 134/85 mmHg, pulse 71 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.6°C, oxygen saturation 99%.
Recent laboratory results: creatinine 3.26 mg/dL, BUN 68 mg/dL, estimated GFR 15 mL/min/1.73 m².

Assessment
Chronic kidney disease with markedly reduced renal function and elevated BUN/creatinine. The patient has persistent fatigue but no reported symptoms of fluid overload or significant uremic prodrome. Disease control appears suboptimal given the current renal indices and absence of an established management regimen. Blood pressure is acceptable at today’s visit. Gout is an important comorbidity in the setting of advanced renal impairment.

Plan
Arrange prompt nephrology evaluation for comprehensive CKD management and discussion of renal replacement therapy planning as clinically appropriate.

Obtain urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum electrolytes, CBC, intact parathyroid hormone, and renal ultrasound as part of disease reassessment.

Review renal-protective measures, including avoidance of NSAIDs and other nephrotoxins, appropriate dietary sodium and protein intake, and maintenance of adequate hydration without excess fluid intake.

Review gout treatment options with attention to renal dosing and avoidance of unsupervised over-the-counter analgesics.

Monitor blood pressure, urinary changes, edema, dyspnea, nausea, pruritus, worsening fatigue, or confusion. Seek urgent evaluation for progressive shortness of breath, marked swelling, persistent vomiting, chest pain, or altered mental status.
