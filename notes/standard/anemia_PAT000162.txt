Chief Complaint

Annual review and follow-up of anemia.

Subjective

George Cunningham is a 53-year-old man presenting for comprehensive annual review and assessment of anemia present for approximately 3 years. He reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, reduced exercise tolerance, shortness of breath, palpitations, or recognized pallor. He does not smoke.

Relevant chronic conditions include inflammatory bowel disease of approximately 1 year’s duration and a history of malignancy of approximately 6 years’ duration. No current anemia-directed treatment is documented. He has no reported anemia-related functional limitation at this visit.

Objective

Height 164 cm; weight 68.9 kg; BMI 25.6 kg/m².

Blood pressure 122/76 mmHg, pulse 82 bpm, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 99%.

Laboratory results:
Hemoglobin 9.4 g/dL
Hematocrit 28.8%
RBC count 3.2 million/µL
MCV 90 fL
Serum iron 36 µg/dL
Ferritin 407 ng/mL
TIBC 221 µg/dL
Transferrin saturation 16.3%

The anemia is normocytic with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and reduced TIBC.

Assessment

Anemia consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/chronic disease. Hemoglobin and hematocrit are reduced with a normocytic pattern. The low serum iron and TIBC with elevated ferritin support impaired iron availability in the setting of chronic disease rather than straightforward iron depletion, although mixed etiologies should be considered. His inflammatory bowel disease and history of malignancy are clinically relevant potential contributors. He is currently asymptomatic from an anemia standpoint, with stable vital signs and normal oxygen saturation.

Inflammatory bowel disease, approximately 1 year in duration, and malignancy history, approximately 6 years in duration. Ongoing disease activity and treatment status should be reviewed with the clinicians managing these conditions, as control of underlying inflammation is central to management of the anemia.

Overweight-range BMI at 25.6 kg/m².

Plan

Review the anemia and laboratory pattern with the patient. Recommend repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear to assess persistence and marrow response. Recommend renal function testing, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers as clinically appropriate. Consider repeat iron studies and evaluation for a mixed iron-deficiency component, particularly in the context of inflammatory bowel disease or any gastrointestinal blood loss.

Recommend coordination with gastroenterology and the clinician overseeing his malignancy history to assess current inflammatory or malignant disease activity and determine whether additional evaluation is indicated. Further iron replacement, including oral or intravenous therapy, should be guided by the underlying disease activity, repeat studies, tolerance, and the treating clinician’s assessment; no current iron or erythropoietin therapy is documented.

Advise prompt assessment for new or worsening shortness of breath, chest discomfort, palpitations, dizziness, syncope, bleeding, or marked decline in exercise tolerance.

Continue annual preventive care, including review of immunizations and age-appropriate cancer screening in the context of his established malignancy history and inflammatory bowel disease. Encourage balanced nutrition, regular physical activity as tolerated, and avoidance of tobacco; he is a nonsmoker.

Arrange outpatient follow-up after repeat evaluation of the anemia and sooner if symptoms develop.
