Chief Complaint

Annual review of type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Brianna Gibson is a 38-year-old woman presenting for comprehensive annual review of type 2 diabetes mellitus diagnosed approximately 3 years ago. Her diabetes has not been actively managed. Recent laboratory results show HbA1c 9.5% and fasting glucose 246 mg/dL, consistent with significant hyperglycemia. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling in the hands or feet.

She does not smoke. Her documented comorbidities include obstructive sleep apnea for 8 years and anxiety for 4 years. No current diabetes medications were provided for review.

Objective

Height 159 cm, weight 55.1 kg, BMI 21.8 kg/m². Blood pressure 119/82 mmHg, pulse 75 bpm, respiratory rate 17/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Available laboratory results: HbA1c 9.5%; fasting glucose 246 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications, currently poorly controlled based on HbA1c 9.5% and fasting glucose 246 mg/dL. Despite marked biochemical hyperglycemia, she is presently without reported polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss, visual symptoms, neuropathic symptoms, recurrent infections, or poor wound healing. Her BMI is within the normal range.

Obstructive sleep apnea, longstanding.

Anxiety, longstanding.

Plan

Discussed the significance of the elevated HbA1c and fasting glucose and the risk of future microvascular and cardiovascular complications. Recommended prompt initiation of an evidence-based diabetes treatment plan, including consideration of metformin if no contraindication, with shared decision-making regarding benefits, adverse effects, and monitoring. Reviewed nutrition, regular physical activity, avoidance of sugar-sweetened beverages, and home glucose monitoring as appropriate. Because the HbA1c is substantially elevated, close follow-up is recommended rather than lifestyle management alone.

Recommend assessment of renal status with serum creatinine/eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, fasting lipid profile, and a comprehensive diabetic foot examination. Recommend a dilated retinal examination for diabetic eye screening. Continue monitoring for polyuria, polydipsia, visual changes, weight loss, neuropathy, skin breakdown, or recurrent infections.

Preventive care reviewed in the context of the annual visit, including age-appropriate immunizations and routine cancer screening based on current preventive-care recommendations. Encourage continued avoidance of tobacco. Follow up soon to review diabetes treatment initiation and home glucose data, with repeat HbA1c after an appropriate interval to assess response.
