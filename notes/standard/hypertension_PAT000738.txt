Plan

Review dietary sodium intake, encourage regular activity as tolerated, weight maintenance, and limitation of alcohol if applicable.

Return for close blood pressure follow-up. Seek emergency care for severe headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, new neurologic symptoms, vision changes, or other acute concerning symptoms.
