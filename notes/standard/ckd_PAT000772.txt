Plan

Recommend prompt nephrology evaluation given the substantially reduced eGFR and elevated BUN. Discuss renal replacement planning and conservative management options as clinically appropriate, without waiting for development of symptoms.

Perform comprehensive medication reconciliation, including prescription, over-the-counter, and herbal products. Avoid nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs and other potentially nephrotoxic agents unless specifically directed by a treating clinician. Any medications should be reviewed for renal dosing and interactions.

Reassess blood pressure and establish or optimize an individualized antihypertensive regimen through the treating clinician. Encourage adherence once a regimen is confirmed and review tolerance at follow-up.

Recommend serum electrolytes, bicarbonate, calcium, phosphorus, CBC, and intact parathyroid hormone assessment to guide management of advanced kidney disease, anemia, and secondary hyperparathyroidism. Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio and renal ultrasound as part of renal evaluation if not recently completed.

Counsel regarding urgent evaluation for new shortness of breath, progressive edema, persistent nausea or vomiting, confusion, marked reduction in urine output, chest pain, or severe weakness.

Follow up promptly after medication reconciliation and renal evaluation.
