Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up for elevated blood pressure.

Subjective

Benjamin Murphy is a 62-year-old man presenting for focused follow-up of longstanding hypertension, present for approximately 30 years and currently without active management. He denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, or epistaxis. Relevant comorbidities include chronic kidney disease of approximately 22 years’ duration and coronary artery disease of approximately 11 years’ duration. He currently smokes. Height 186 cm, weight 95.8 kg, BMI 27.7 kg/m².

Objective

Blood pressure 164/94 mmHg. Pulse 75 bpm. Respiratory rate 15/min. Temperature 36.6°C. Oxygen saturation 95%.

Serum electrolytes: sodium 140 mmol/L, potassium 4.3 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension with persistently elevated blood pressure and no reported symptoms of acute end-organ involvement. Long duration without active management, with increased cardiovascular and renal risk given known coronary artery disease, chronic kidney disease, and ongoing tobacco use.

Plan

Confirm blood pressure with repeat measurement using appropriate technique and recommend home blood pressure monitoring with a written log.

Arrange prompt outpatient medication reconciliation and initiation or adjustment of antihypertensive therapy, taking chronic kidney disease and coronary artery disease into account. Review renal function and urine testing as clinically appropriate before and during treatment; current sodium and potassium are within normal limits.

Recommend a lower-sodium eating pattern, regular physical activity as tolerated, weight management, and smoking cessation. Counsel regarding adherence once therapy is established.

Recommend ECG and cardiovascular risk assessment, including lipid profile, as clinically appropriate.

Seek emergency care for severe headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, new neurologic symptoms, vision loss, or markedly worsening blood pressure. Arrange close primary care follow-up for blood pressure reassessment.
