Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up for markedly elevated blood pressure.

Subjective

Kelsey Porter is a 77-year-old woman presenting for follow-up of hypertension, present for approximately 6 years and currently without active management. Blood pressure today is markedly elevated. She reports palpitations and fatigue. She denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, or nosebleeds. Relevant comorbidities include type 2 diabetes mellitus for 15 years and chronic kidney disease for 31 years. She currently smokes. Weight is 78.5 kg with BMI 29.2 kg/m².

Objective

Vital signs: BP 171/100 mmHg, pulse 76 bpm, respirations 17/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 95% on room air. Height 164 cm; weight 78.5 kg; BMI 29.2 kg/m².

Recent serum electrolytes: sodium 147 mmol/L, potassium 4.3 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential primary hypertension with markedly elevated reading and no reported symptoms of acute end-organ injury. Hypertension is currently untreated/not actively managed. Increased cardiovascular and renal risk in the setting of advanced age, diabetes, chronic kidney disease, overweight status, and active smoking.

Palpitations and fatigue.

Plan

Arrange prompt same-day clinical reassessment of blood pressure and cardiovascular status. Discuss initiation of an individualized antihypertensive regimen, with medication selection and dosing guided by chronic kidney disease, diabetes, repeat blood pressure measurements, and renal function monitoring. Do not abruptly lower blood pressure without appropriate evaluation.

Recommend emergency evaluation for chest pain, shortness of breath, severe headache, neurologic changes, new visual symptoms, confusion, weakness, or persistent worsening palpitations.

Review home blood pressure monitoring technique and have patient record readings for follow-up. Counsel on reducing dietary sodium, avoiding excess alcohol, increasing physical activity as tolerated, weight reduction, and smoking cessation. Continue close follow-up for blood pressure control and monitoring of electrolytes and kidney function.
