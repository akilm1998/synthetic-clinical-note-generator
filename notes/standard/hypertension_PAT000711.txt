Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up for elevated blood pressure.

Subjective

Sadie Hunter is a 94-year-old woman with a 31-year history of essential hypertension and coronary artery disease present for approximately 44 years. Hypertension has not been actively managed. She denies headache, dizziness, blurred vision, chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, and epistaxis. She does not smoke.

Objective

Blood pressure 165/99 mmHg. Pulse 79 bpm. Respiratory rate 19/min. Temperature 36.8°C. Oxygen saturation 97%. Height 180 cm; weight 87.8 kg; BMI 27.1 kg/m².

Serum electrolytes: sodium 145 mmol/L, potassium 3.7 mmol/L.

Assessment

Essential hypertension with persistently elevated office blood pressure in an asymptomatic 94-year-old woman whose hypertension has not been managed. No reported symptoms concerning for acute hypertensive emergency today. Longstanding coronary artery disease increases the importance of careful blood pressure control and avoidance of overly rapid reduction.

Overweight by BMI.

Plan

Confirm blood pressure with repeat measurements using appropriate cuff and technique; recommend home blood pressure monitoring and documentation of readings. Given the degree of elevation and longstanding untreated hypertension, discuss prompt initiation of an individualized antihypertensive regimen, with conservative titration and close monitoring because of advanced age and coexisting coronary artery disease.

Review dietary sodium reduction, regular activity as tolerated, weight maintenance, and avoidance of excess alcohol. Monitor renal function and electrolytes after treatment is initiated; current sodium and potassium are within acceptable ranges for outpatient management.

Recommend baseline cardiovascular risk assessment, including ECG, lipid profile, and urinalysis, if not recently completed. Advise immediate emergency evaluation for chest pain, new shortness of breath, severe headache, neurologic symptoms, vision change, or markedly higher blood pressure readings. Arrange close outpatient follow-up for blood pressure reassessment and treatment adjustment.
