Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective
Colin Graham is a 70-year-old man seen for urgent follow-up of anemia present for approximately 20 years. He reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, reduced exercise tolerance, dyspnea, palpitations, or pallor. He has an 8-year history of heart failure. He is a current smoker. Height 185 cm, weight 102 kg, BMI 29.8 kg/m². No anemia-directed treatment is documented.

Objective
Vital signs: BP 130/75 mmHg, pulse 88 bpm, respiratory rate 21/min, temperature 36.8°C, oxygen saturation 96%.

Laboratory results:
Hemoglobin 8.1 g/dL
Hematocrit 24.0%
RBC count 2.89 million/µL
MCV 83 fL
Serum iron 29 µg/dL
Ferritin 187 ng/mL
TIBC 254 µg/dL
Transferrin saturation 11.4%

Assessment
Chronic, currently untreated anemia with significant reduction in hemoglobin and hematocrit. The normocytic indices, low serum iron and transferrin saturation, relatively preserved ferritin, and low TIBC are most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/chronic disease, in the context of longstanding disease and heart failure. The degree of anemia warrants prompt reassessment despite the absence of reported cardiopulmonary or constitutional symptoms. Iron-restricted erythropoiesis may coexist and should be further characterized.

Plan
Arrange prompt repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear to confirm severity and assess marrow response.

Recommend evaluation of potential contributors, including renal function, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers; reassess iron indices as clinically indicated.

Review for occult blood loss and other causes of chronic anemia, with consideration of hematology evaluation if the anemia persists or worsens.

Given hemoglobin of 8.1 g/dL and underlying heart failure, advise immediate emergency evaluation for new or worsening dyspnea, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, dizziness, marked weakness, or reduced exercise tolerance.

Address smoking cessation and continue close outpatient follow-up after repeat laboratory assessment.
