Type 2 diabetes mellitus, longstanding, increasing overall cardiovascular risk.

Coronary artery disease, diagnosed 1 year ago, without reported angina or dyspnea today.

Hyperlipidemia, diagnosed 2 years ago.

Overweight, with BMI 29.3 kg/m².

Plan

Recommend assessment of cardiovascular risk factors, including lipid profile and urinalysis, and obtain an ECG as clinically appropriate. Continue coordinated management of diabetes, hyperlipidemia, and coronary artery disease.

Reviewed warning symptoms requiring urgent evaluation, including chest pain, new shortness of breath, severe headache, focal neurologic symptoms, or acute visual changes.

Follow up after home blood pressure readings and medication reconciliation to reassess control and establish an ongoing treatment plan.
