Chief Complaint

Initial outpatient consultation for markedly elevated cholesterol.

History of Present Illness

Faith Hawkins is a 63-year-old woman presenting for initial evaluation and management of longstanding hyperlipidemia, present for approximately 29 years and currently without active treatment. Recent lipid testing shows total cholesterol 360 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 233 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 50 mg/dL, and triglycerides 384 mg/dL. She denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain or intermittent claudication, dizziness, xanthomas, and xanthelasma.

She has had obesity for approximately 8 years and weighs 74.0 kg at a height of 154 cm, with a BMI of 31.2 kg/m². She also has chronic kidney disease of approximately 8 years’ duration. She does not smoke. No lipid-lowering medications are reported.

Objective

Vital signs: Blood pressure 134/87 mmHg, pulse 81 bpm, respiratory rate 18/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 99% on room air.

Height 154 cm. Weight 74.0 kg. BMI 31.2 kg/m².

Laboratory data: Total cholesterol 360 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 233 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 50 mg/dL, triglycerides 384 mg/dL.

No xanthomas or xanthelasma are reported. No cardiovascular or neurologic symptoms are reported today.

Assessment

Longstanding untreated hyperlipidemia with markedly elevated LDL cholesterol and elevated triglycerides. The LDL of 233 mg/dL is substantially above the threshold at which pharmacologic lipid-lowering therapy is generally recommended, independent of calculated short-term risk. The triglyceride level of 384 mg/dL is also elevated and increases overall atherosclerotic risk, although she has no reported symptoms suggestive of established coronary or peripheral arterial disease. Her age, obesity, and chronic kidney disease further increase cardiovascular risk. There is no current clinical history of chest discomfort, claudication, dizziness, xanthomas, or xanthelasma.

Obesity, BMI 31.2 kg/m², present for approximately 8 years and likely contributing to the adverse lipid profile.

Chronic kidney disease, present for approximately 8 years. Renal function and albuminuria data are not included in today’s laboratory information; kidney status will be important in selecting and monitoring therapy.

Plan

Discussed the significance of the lipid results and the importance of active treatment given the severity and duration of the dyslipidemia. Recommend initiation of a high-intensity statin after review of renal function, medication interactions, contraindications, and shared decision-making. If LDL cholesterol remains substantially elevated despite adherence and tolerated statin therapy, addition of ezetimibe can be considered. Treatment should be coordinated with the patient’s kidney disease status.

Recommend a fasting lipid panel for confirmation and baseline monitoring, along with assessment for potentially contributing secondary causes and medication safety, including liver enzymes, renal function, thyroid function, and glycemic evaluation as clinically appropriate. Consider measurement of apolipoprotein B and lipoprotein(a) for further cardiovascular risk characterization. A coronary artery calcium scan is not required before addressing an LDL level of this magnitude but may be discussed later if treatment decisions remain uncertain.

Counseled regarding a heart-healthy eating pattern emphasizing vegetables, fruits, legumes, whole grains, lean proteins, and unsaturated fats while reducing saturated and trans fats, refined carbohydrates, and concentrated sugars. Recommend gradual weight reduction, regular aerobic and resistance activity as tolerated, and avoidance of excess alcohol, which may worsen triglyceride elevation. She remains a nonsmoker.

Because of chronic kidney disease, advised ongoing monitoring of renal function and avoidance of unreviewed over-the-counter or herbal products. Reviewed symptoms that should prompt urgent evaluation, including new chest pressure, shortness of breath, or sudden exercise-related leg pain.

Follow up after repeat fasting laboratory testing and initiation or adjustment of lipid-lowering therapy to assess adherence, tolerance, and lipid response.
