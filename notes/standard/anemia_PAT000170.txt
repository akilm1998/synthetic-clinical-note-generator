Chief Complaint

Urgent follow-up for chronic anemia.

Subjective

Samuel Wood is a 92-year-old man presenting for focused follow-up of anemia documented for approximately 21 years. He reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, reduced exercise tolerance, shortness of breath, palpitations, or other acute anemia-related symptoms. No pallor has been reported.

Relevant chronic conditions include inflammatory bowel disease for 9 years, rheumatoid arthritis for 9 years, and chronic infection for 59 years. He currently smokes. No treatment regimen for the anemia is documented.

Objective

Height 177 cm; weight 73.6 kg; BMI 23.5 kg/m².

Blood pressure 132/81 mmHg, pulse 86 bpm, respiratory rate 21/min, temperature 36.7°C, oxygen saturation 95%.

Hemoglobin 8.3 g/dL, hematocrit 25.0%, RBC 3.05 million/µL, MCV 82 fL. Serum iron 34 µg/dL, ferritin 418 ng/mL, TIBC 215 µg/dL, transferrin saturation 15.8%.

Assessment

Chronic anemia consistent with anemia of chronic disease/inflammation. The anemia is significant, with hemoglobin 8.3 g/dL and hematocrit 25.0%. Indices are near normocytic, with low serum iron and transferrin saturation, elevated ferritin, and low TIBC, supporting inflammatory iron restriction in the setting of longstanding inflammatory bowel disease, rheumatoid arthritis, and chronic infection. A concurrent iron-deficient component or other contributor cannot be excluded based on the available data. He is currently hemodynamically stable and denies cardiopulmonary or constitutional symptoms.

Plan

Recommend prompt repeat clinical and laboratory assessment given the degree and chronicity of anemia, including CBC with reticulocyte count, peripheral smear, renal function testing, vitamin B12, folate, and inflammatory markers. Reassess iron status and evaluate for clinically apparent or occult blood loss, particularly given his inflammatory bowel disease. Consider hematology consultation for further evaluation and treatment planning, including assessment of appropriateness for iron replacement or an erythropoiesis-stimulating agent based on the underlying inflammatory and renal status.

Discussed urgent evaluation for new shortness of breath, chest pain, palpitations, syncope, worsening weakness, dizziness, bleeding, or other acute change. Smoking cessation encouraged.
