Chief Complaint
Urgent follow-up for markedly elevated cholesterol.

Subjective
Tristan Burke is a 36-year-old male presenting for follow-up of hyperlipidemia, present for approximately 3 years and not currently under active treatment. He denies chest discomfort, exertional leg pain, dizziness, xanthomas, or xanthelasma. He does not smoke. History is notable for obesity of approximately 9 years’ duration. Current weight is 96.2 kg at 170 cm (BMI 33.3 kg/m²).

Objective
Vital signs: BP 115/82 mmHg, pulse 78 bpm, respiratory rate 21/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 98%.

Laboratory results: Total cholesterol 331 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 239 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 37 mg/dL, triglycerides 275 mg/dL.

Assessment
Hyperlipidemia with markedly elevated LDL cholesterol and elevated triglycerides, currently untreated. The LDL level is associated with increased lifetime atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk despite absence of current cardiopulmonary or claudication symptoms. Low HDL and obesity are additional adverse metabolic risk factors.

Obesity, BMI 33.3 kg/m².

Plan
Discussed the severity of the lipid abnormalities and the importance of treatment to reduce long-term cardiovascular risk. Recommend initiation of a high-intensity statin after shared decision-making; reviewed general benefits, potential adverse effects, and the need to report significant muscle symptoms or dark urine. Obtain evaluation for secondary contributors, including thyroid, glycemic, hepatic, and renal abnormalities, as clinically appropriate. Consider measurement of lipoprotein(a) and apolipoprotein B to further refine risk assessment, and clarify family history of premature cardiovascular disease.

Counseled on a heart-healthy eating pattern with reduction of saturated and trans fats, refined carbohydrates, and excess calories; increase dietary fiber and routine physical activity as tolerated. Encourage gradual weight reduction.

Repeat fasting lipid panel after treatment initiation and lifestyle changes to assess response. Return for close outpatient follow-up to review results and confirm implementation of therapy.
