Chief Complaint

Annual review of type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Adriana Wells is a 40-year-old woman presenting for comprehensive annual review. She has a 5-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus that is currently not actively managed. Recent laboratory results show HbA1c 9.4% and fasting glucose 228 mg/dL. She denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, recurrent infections, numbness, or tingling of the hands or feet.

She has obstructive sleep apnea, present for approximately 6 years. She reports current tobacco use. No medication regimen is provided for review today.

Objective

Vital signs: BP 118/79 mmHg, pulse 77 bpm, respiratory rate 18/min, temperature 36.9°C, oxygen saturation 98% on room air.

Height 174 cm, weight 85.1 kg, BMI 28.1 kg/m².

Available laboratory results: HbA1c 9.4%; fasting glucose 228 mg/dL.

No acute distress or diabetes-related symptoms reported. No additional examination findings documented today.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without reported complications. Glycemic control is significantly above goal based on HbA1c 9.4% and fasting glucose 228 mg/dL. She is currently asymptomatic but has had diabetes for 5 years without an active management plan. No neuropathic, infectious, visual, or constitutional symptoms reported.

Overweight, BMI 28.1 kg/m².

Obstructive sleep apnea, 6-year history.

Current tobacco use.

Plan

Discussed the importance of active diabetes management and the risks of persistent hyperglycemia, including eye, kidney, nerve, and cardiovascular complications. Recommend prompt treatment planning, including initiation of appropriate glucose-lowering medication after medication reconciliation and review of renal function. Metformin would be a reasonable first-line consideration if no contraindication is identified; treatment should be individualized based on follow-up assessment and laboratory evaluation.

Recommend dietary counseling focused on reducing sugar-sweetened beverages and refined carbohydrates, portion control, increased fiber, and a balanced meal pattern. Encourage gradual regular physical activity, with a goal of at least 150 minutes weekly as tolerated, along with continued weight reduction.

Recommend repeat HbA1c after approximately 3 months of active treatment. Obtain urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine with estimated glomerular filtration rate, and fasting lipid profile for diabetes health maintenance and cardiovascular risk assessment. Recommend a comprehensive diabetic foot examination and dilated retinal examination. Advise prompt evaluation for new vision changes, persistent hyperglycemic symptoms, foot wounds, or signs of infection.

Counseled regarding smoking cessation and offered support with a quit plan and cessation therapies at follow-up.

Review obstructive sleep apnea management at the next visit and reinforce adherence to prescribed therapy if applicable; avoid assuming current treatment.

Recommend routine preventive care appropriate for age, including review of immunizations and age-appropriate cancer screening. Follow up soon to establish a concrete diabetes treatment plan, review recommended testing, and reassess glycemic control.
