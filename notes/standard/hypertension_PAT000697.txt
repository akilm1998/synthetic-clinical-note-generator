Routine follow-up for hypertension.

Assessment

Essential hypertension with blood pressure above goal at today’s visit in the setting of longstanding disease and significant cardiovascular comorbidity. Plan

Discussed the importance of active blood pressure management and reduction of cardiovascular risk.
