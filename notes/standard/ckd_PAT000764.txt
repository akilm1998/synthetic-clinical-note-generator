Assessment

Chronic kidney disease with substantially reduced renal filtration, based on creatinine 3.36 mg/dL, BUN 79 mg/dL, and eGFR 18 mL/min/1.73 m². The disease has been present for approximately 5 years and is currently clinically asymptomatic by history, without reported edema, dyspnea, nausea, pruritus, muscle cramps, sleep disturbance, or urinary frequency change. Etiology is not established from the available information. Given the degree of renal impairment and the absence of active management documented at this visit, he requires structured nephrology evaluation and renal-protective care.

Anemia of chronic disease, present for approximately 4 years; current hemoglobin and iron studies are not available.

Coronary artery disease, present for approximately 1 year, increasing overall cardiovascular risk in the setting of chronic kidney disease.

Borderline elevated blood pressure at today’s visit, 135/90 mmHg.

Active tobacco use.

Plan

Arrange expedited nephrology consultation for comprehensive evaluation and longitudinal management of advanced chronic kidney disease, including review of renal trajectory and discussion of renal replacement planning if clinically indicated by subsequent assessment.

Recommend urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, serum electrolytes, bicarbonate, calcium, phosphorus, complete blood count, iron studies, and intact parathyroid hormone as part of CKD evaluation. Recommend renal ultrasound to assess renal size and structural abnormalities. These studies should be interpreted alongside prior renal laboratory trends when available.

Monitor renal function and blood pressure closely. Avoid nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, iodinated contrast when alternatives are appropriate, and other potential nephrotoxins. Medication dosing should be reviewed for renal adjustment, particularly in the context of coronary artery disease and reduced eGFR. No medication regimen is documented today; treatment changes should be based on complete medication reconciliation and laboratory review.

Counsel regarding a kidney-protective lifestyle, including avoidance of excess dietary sodium, moderation of protein intake rather than high-protein diets, maintenance of healthy weight, and avoidance of dehydration. BMI is currently 25.4 kg/m².

Strongly advise smoking cessation given the combined renal and cardiovascular risk.

Review warning symptoms requiring urgent evaluation, including new or worsening shortness of breath, rapidly progressive edema, persistent nausea or vomiting, confusion, marked reduction in urine output, or chest pain.

Follow up after completion of the recommended evaluation, or sooner for any new symptoms.
