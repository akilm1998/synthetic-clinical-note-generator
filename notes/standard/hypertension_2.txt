Assessment: Essential hypertension, currently reasonably controlled on today’s reading without symptoms suggestive of hypertensive crisis. Dizziness, palpitations, and fatigue require evaluation given advanced age and underlying coronary artery disease and chronic kidney disease. Electrolytes are within the provided reference context, with potassium at the upper end of normal. Active tobacco use increases cardiovascular risk.

Plan: Continue current hypertension management and reinforce adherence; medication reconciliation is recommended before any adjustment. Encourage home blood pressure monitoring with a record of readings and symptoms, avoiding excessive dietary sodium and maintaining appropriate hydration. Recommend an ECG to evaluate palpitations and review of a lipid profile and urinalysis as part of cardiovascular and renal risk assessment. Continue monitoring renal function and electrolytes in the context of chronic kidney disease and antihypertensive therapy. Counsel regarding smoking cessation. Close outpatient follow-up after ECG and symptom review.
