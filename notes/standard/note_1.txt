Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Charlie Brown is a 53-year-old man with a 16-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, here for routine outpatient follow-up. He reports that his diabetes has been well controlled on his current regimen and that he takes his medications consistently. He does not routinely check glucose every day but reports home readings generally between 100 and 150 mg/dL. He denies increased thirst, frequent urination, polyphagia, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, or recurrent infections. No recent hypoglycemic episodes.

He denies numbness, tingling, burning pain, or wounds of the feet. He has not had chest pain, shortness of breath, or exercise intolerance. He does not smoke. History is also notable for longstanding obesity and depression. Mood is stable on current treatment, and he denies suicidal or homicidal thoughts.

Objective

Vital Signs: Blood pressure 128/78 mmHg, pulse 72 beats/min, respiratory rate 16/min, temperature 98.1°F, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Weight 246 lb, BMI 35.3 kg/m².

General: Well-appearing, pleasant, and in no acute distress.

HEENT: Mucous membranes moist. No scleral icterus.

Cardiovascular: Regular rate and rhythm. No murmurs. Peripheral pulses palpable.

Respiratory: Lungs clear bilaterally. No respiratory distress.

Abdomen: Soft and nontender.

Extremities: No edema. Skin intact without ulcers or calluses.

Diabetic foot examination: Dorsalis pedis pulses 2+ bilaterally. Normal skin temperature and color. Protective sensation intact bilaterally to monofilament testing. No deformity or open lesions.

Neurologic: Alert and oriented. Gait normal.

Psychiatric: Cooperative with appropriate affect. Thought process logical.

Recent laboratory results: Hemoglobin A1c 6.8%, fasting glucose 124 mg/dL, creatinine 0.9 mg/dL, estimated glomerular filtration rate 98 mL/min/1.73 m², urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio 8 mg/g. Total cholesterol 164 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 88 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 48 mg/dL, triglycerides 141 mg/dL.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications, currently well controlled. No evidence of neuropathy, nephropathy, or active foot complications.

Obesity, longstanding.

Depression, currently stable by history.

Plan

Continue current diabetes treatment and home glucose monitoring. Reinforced adherence to medications, nutrition plan, portion control, and regular physical activity with a goal of at least 150 minutes per week as tolerated.

Reviewed signs and symptoms of hypoglycemia and advised the patient to contact the office for recurrent low or persistently elevated glucose readings.

Continue routine diabetic foot care, daily self-inspection of the feet, and properly fitting footwear. Recommend annual dilated retinal examination; patient will schedule if not completed within the past year.

Renal function and urine albumin are currently normal. Repeat hemoglobin A1c, metabolic panel, lipid panel, and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio at the next routine interval.

Continue current depression management and follow up sooner for worsening mood or safety concerns.

Return for diabetes follow-up in 3 to 4 months.
