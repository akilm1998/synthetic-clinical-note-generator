Chief Complaint

Routine follow-up of chronic anemia.

Subjective

Faith Hawkins is a 58-year-old woman presenting for outpatient follow-up of anemia present for approximately 28 years. She reports no current fatigue, weakness, dizziness, headache, palpitations, shortness of breath, or reduced exercise tolerance. No pallor is reported. She has a history of malignancy for approximately 18 years and heart failure for 3 years. She currently smokes. No anemia-directed treatment is documented.

Objective

Height 151 cm; weight 60.0 kg; BMI 26.3 kg/m².

Blood pressure 124/76 mmHg, pulse 88 bpm, respiratory rate 23/min, temperature 37.0°C, oxygen saturation 96%.

Laboratory results:
Hemoglobin 8.0 g/dL
Hematocrit 23.9%
RBC 2.63 million/µL
MCV 91 fL
Serum iron 27 µg/dL
Ferritin 295 ng/mL
TIBC 247 µg/dL
Transferrin saturation 10.9%

Assessment

Chronic normocytic anemia with significant reduction in hemoglobin and hematocrit. Low serum iron and transferrin saturation with elevated ferritin and low TIBC are most consistent with anemia of chronic inflammation/chronic disease, in the setting of longstanding malignancy and heart failure. She is currently clinically stable and denies cardiopulmonary or constitutional symptoms. No formal disease stage assigned.

Tobacco use.

Plan

Repeat CBC with reticulocyte count and peripheral smear to assess persistence and marrow response.

Obtain vitamin B12, folate, renal function, and inflammatory markers as clinically appropriate to evaluate additional reversible contributors.

Review for occult blood loss and nutritional or gastrointestinal contributors despite the currently inflammatory-appearing iron profile.

Coordinate anemia management with the clinicians managing her malignancy and heart failure. Consider hematology evaluation and discussion of iron replacement or erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy based on the underlying disease, renal function, symptoms, and repeat laboratory assessment; no treatment is initiated or assumed today.

Discuss smoking cessation.

Seek urgent evaluation for new or worsening shortness of breath, chest pain, syncope, palpitations, marked weakness, or bleeding.
