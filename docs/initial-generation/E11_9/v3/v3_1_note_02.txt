Chief Complaint

Follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Alice Mathew is a 51-year-old woman presenting for routine diabetes follow-up. She reports taking metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals and generally tolerating it, with occasional loose stools. Home glucose readings are typically 110–145 mg/dL fasting and 140–180 mg/dL after meals. She denies symptomatic hypoglycemia, polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia, unintentional weight loss, blurred vision, chest pain, dyspnea, or foot ulcers. She reports occasional fatigue but no numbness, burning, or weakness in the feet.

She follows a generally low-carbohydrate diet but acknowledges limited regular exercise. Weight has been stable. She continues to smoke approximately 5 cigarettes daily and is interested in reducing use. She uses CPAP inconsistently for obstructive sleep apnea. Depression is described as stable; she denies suicidal ideation.

Objective

Vital signs: Blood pressure 128/78 mmHg, pulse 76 beats/minute, respiratory rate 16/minute, temperature 98.1°F, oxygen saturation 97% on room air. Weight 194 lb, BMI 32.3 kg/m².

General: Alert, oriented, and in no acute distress. Mild central obesity.

Cardiovascular: Regular rate and rhythm. No murmurs. Peripheral pulses 2+ and symmetric.

Respiratory: Clear to auscultation bilaterally. No wheezes or crackles.

Abdomen: Soft and nontender.

Skin: No ulcers or concerning lesions. No significant acanthosis noted.

Diabetic foot examination: Skin intact without fissures, calluses, or ulcers. Warm feet with brisk capillary refill. Dorsalis pedis and posterior tibial pulses 2+ bilaterally. Sensation intact to monofilament testing bilaterally.

Laboratory results: Hemoglobin A1c 7.2%, previously 7.4%. Fasting glucose 132 mg/dL. Creatinine 0.8 mg/dL with estimated GFR 88 mL/min/1.73 m². Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio 9 mg/g. Total cholesterol 182 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 103 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol 49 mg/dL, triglycerides 148 mg/dL. Liver enzymes within normal limits.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications, currently reasonably controlled with metformin and lifestyle measures. No evidence of hypoglycemia, nephropathy, neuropathy, or diabetic foot complications on today’s evaluation.

Depression, clinically stable by history.

Obstructive sleep apnea with inconsistent CPAP use.

Tobacco use.

Plan

Continue metformin 1000 mg twice daily with meals. Reinforced nutrition counseling focused on portion control, increased fiber, avoidance of sugar-sweetened beverages, and limiting refined carbohydrates. Encourage gradual increase in physical activity toward at least 150 minutes weekly as tolerated.

Continue home glucose monitoring, particularly fasting and occasionally 2 hours after meals. Reviewed symptoms and treatment of hypoglycemia.

Repeat hemoglobin A1c in approximately 3 months. Monitor renal function and urine albumin annually.

Recommend annual dilated retinal examination; patient will schedule this with her eye care provider.

Continue daily foot inspection and protective footwear. Discussed prompt evaluation for wounds, redness, drainage, or loss of sensation.

Counseled regarding smoking cessation; encouraged setting a quit date and offered pharmacologic and behavioral support.

Encouraged consistent nightly CPAP use and follow-up with sleep medicine if mask tolerance or equipment issues persist.

Return to clinic in 3 months or sooner for persistent glucose elevation, hypoglycemia, new neurologic symptoms, or foot problems.
