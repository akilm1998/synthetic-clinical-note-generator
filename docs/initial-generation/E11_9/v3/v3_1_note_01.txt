Chief Complaint

Follow-up for type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Subjective

Jane Watson is a 33-year-old woman presenting for routine diabetes follow-up. She reports taking metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals and tolerating it without significant gastrointestinal symptoms. She does not routinely check home glucose readings. Denies increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unintentional weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, chest pain, dyspnea, or symptoms of hypoglycemia. No numbness, tingling, wounds, or slow-healing sores of the feet. She reports limited exercise and acknowledges that her diet has included more refined carbohydrates recently. She smokes cigarettes daily and is interested in reducing use but is not ready to set a quit date.

No known hypertension, dyslipidemia, kidney disease, or other chronic medical conditions. No recent emergency visits or hospitalizations.

Objective

Vital signs: Blood pressure 124/78 mmHg, pulse 82 beats/minute, respiratory rate 16/minute, temperature 98.2°F, oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Weight 186 lb, height 64 in, BMI 31.9 kg/m².

General: Alert, well appearing, in no acute distress.

Cardiovascular: Regular rate and rhythm. No murmurs, rubs, or gallops.

Respiratory: Clear to auscultation bilaterally. No wheezes or crackles.

Abdomen: Soft and nontender.

Extremities: No edema. Dorsalis pedis and posterior tibial pulses 2+ bilaterally. Feet warm and intact without calluses, ulcers, or skin breakdown.

Neurologic: Sensation to monofilament intact bilaterally. Normal gait.

Skin: No diabetic dermopathy or acanthosis noted on exposed skin.

Recent laboratory results: Hemoglobin A1c 6.9%, fasting glucose 126 mg/dL, creatinine 0.74 mg/dL, estimated GFR 108 mL/min/1.73 m², urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio 8 mg/g. Total cholesterol 182 mg/dL, LDL cholesterol 108 mg/dL, HDL cholesterol  Fifty-two mg/dL, triglycerides 122 mg/dL. Liver enzymes within normal limits.

Assessment

Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications. Glycemic control is near goal on metformin therapy. Renal function and urine albumin excretion are normal. Foot examination is normal, with no evidence of neuropathy or ulceration.

Plan

Continue metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals. Reinforced nutrition focused on vegetables, lean proteins, high-fiber carbohydrates, portion control, and avoidance of sugar-sweetened beverages. Encourage gradual increase in physical activity toward at least 150 minutes weekly, as tolerated.

Recommend checking fasting glucose several times weekly and keeping a log for review. Repeat hemoglobin A1c in approximately 3 months. Repeat comprehensive metabolic panel, fasting lipid profile, and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio at routine follow-up.

Continue annual dilated retinal examination; patient was advised to schedule this if not completed within the past year. Reviewed daily foot inspection and appropriate footwear.

Discussed smoking cessation, including health benefits and available nicotine replacement and prescription therapies. Patient will consider reduction and cessation resources.

Return in 3 months for diabetes follow-up, or sooner for persistent glucose elevations, hypoglycemia, vision changes, neuropathy symptoms, or foot lesions.
